How Auto Budget works

Read this once

You tell us your monthly spend cap (e.g. $3,000) and the min/max daily budget for any campaign. We monitor performance and automatically nudge campaign budgets up or down — daily, weekly or hourly — so you finish the month near your cap, with money going to whichever campaigns are converting best.
