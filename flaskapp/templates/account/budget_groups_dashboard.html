Why use budget groups?

Quick read

A budget group is a separate monthly cap with its own settings. Example: $2,000/mo for HVAC, $1,000/mo for Plumbing. You can assign specific campaigns to each group, and Auto Budget will manage them independently. Anything not in a group uses your main Auto Budget settings.
