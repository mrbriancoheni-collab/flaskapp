What's your growth goal right now?

This tells our AI whether to push hard for volume, cut waste, or stay steady — it changes how it makes decisions every week.

A couple of sentences helps our AI write better ad copy and understand who your best customers are.

No need to be formal — just describe what you do and who you help.

Include seasonal or specialty services too — the more detail, the better the targeting.

Sometimes you're full, or a service isn't profitable enough to advertise. Tell us what to leave out so we don't attract jobs you can't take or don't want.
