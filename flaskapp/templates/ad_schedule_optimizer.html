How to Apply This in Google Ads (5 Steps)

  1. 1

    Open your campaign in Google Ads

    Go to Campaigns → [Your Campaign] → Settings → Ad Schedule.

  2. 2

    Click "+ Ad Schedule" and add time blocks

    Add each time block from the grid above as a separate row. Use the exact day/hour ranges shown in your downloaded schedule.

  3. 3

    Set bid adjustments per block

    Click the pencil icon on each row → set the bid adjustment. Use +20% for dark green blocks, 0% for light green, −20% for yellow. Remove gray-period rows entirely.

  4. 4

    Save and monitor for 2 weeks

    Give the schedule at least 14 days of data before adjusting. Compare conversion rate by hour in Reports → Predefined Reports → Time → Hour of day.

  5. 5

    Refine based on your own data

    After 30 days, cross-reference with your CRM call logs. If jobs are coming in at 8pm, adjust that block from yellow to standard.
