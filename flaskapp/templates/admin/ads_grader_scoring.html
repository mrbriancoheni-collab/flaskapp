Extra Waste Penalty (Applied to Overall Score)

When actual waste percentage exceeds 10%, an additional deduction is applied after the weighted sum:

extra_penalty = (waste_pct − 10) × 2
overall_score = weighted_sum − extra_penalty

Example: 20% actual waste → −(20−10)×2 = −20 pts on top of the 25% dimension weight

Waste % is derived from the wasted spend score: waste_pct = (100 − score) / 2.5
