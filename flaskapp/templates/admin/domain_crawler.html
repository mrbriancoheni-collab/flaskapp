How Domain Crawling Works

1. Identifies Contacts: Finds CRM contacts with domains that need enrichment

2. Crawls Websites: Visits common pages (homepage, contact, about) on each domain

3. Extracts Information: Finds and validates phone numbers and email addresses

4. Updates CRM: Enriches contact records with discovered information

Privacy Note: Only crawls publicly visible contact pages. Respects robots.txt and only enriches opt-in business leads.

Automated Daily Crawling

To enable automated daily crawling, set up a cron job on your server:

# Add to crontab (runs daily at 2 AM)
0 2 * * * cd /home/fieljtgr/flaskapp && python3 -c "from app import create_app; from app.tasks.domain_crawler_task import run_daily_crawler; app = create_app(); app.app_context().push(); run_daily_crawler(max_domains=50)"

This will automatically crawl up to 50 domains each night to keep your CRM enriched.
