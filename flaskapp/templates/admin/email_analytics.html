Tips for Better Email Performance

Improve Open Rates:

  • Personalize subject lines with recipient names
  • Keep subject lines under 50 characters
  • Avoid spam trigger words ("FREE", "ACT NOW")
  • Test different send times (Tue-Thu, 10am-2pm works best)
  • Use numbers and questions in subject lines

Improve Click Rates:

  • Include clear, prominent call-to-action buttons
  • Keep email content focused on one main goal
  • Use compelling button text ("Get Started" vs "Click Here")
  • Add urgency or scarcity when appropriate
  • Mobile-optimize all links and buttons
