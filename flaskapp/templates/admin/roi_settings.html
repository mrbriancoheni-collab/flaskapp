📚 How to Use These Settings

Improvement Rates: These percentages are based on industry benchmarks. More conservative estimates (lower %) build trust, while higher estimates show more potential value.

Pricing Strategy: Most agencies charge 15-30% of savings. Charging 20% means clients save $4 for every $1 they pay you (4x ROI), which is very attractive.

Quality Score Impact: Google's data shows each Quality Score point improvement reduces CPC by approximately 16%. This is well-documented and defensible.

Updating Settings: Changes take effect immediately for new reports and recommendations. Existing reports retain their original calculations.
