How Capacity-Based Ads Optimization Works

1. Data Collection: We sync your jobs, appointments, and technician availability from ServiceTitan.

2. Capacity Calculation: We calculate daily capacity based on scheduled jobs vs. available technician slots.

3. Budget Recommendation: When you have high capacity (low utilization), we recommend increasing ad spend to fill available slots. When capacity is low (high utilization), we recommend reducing spend to prevent overbooking.

4. Automated Optimization: Our AI agents can automatically adjust your Google Ads budgets based on these capacity insights.
