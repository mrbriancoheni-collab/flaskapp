Common Questions

Why is this free?

We offer the grader free because it shows you exactly what's wrong with your account. If the results are bad enough, some business owners hire us to fix it. Either way, you leave with real data.

Will you make changes to my account?

No. We request read-only access through Google's official OAuth. We cannot make changes even if we wanted to. You can revoke access from your Google Account settings at any time.

What happens after the test?

You get a full report with a score for every area, the estimated dollar amount you're wasting, and specific things to fix. If you want the AI to fix them automatically, that's available on our paid plans.

What if my score is embarrassingly low?

That's actually a good thing — it means there's more money to recover. Most accounts we test score between 40–60. Anything under 60 is costing you real money every day.
