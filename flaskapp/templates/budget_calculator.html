How This Calculator Works

This calculator uses industry cost-per-lead (CPL) benchmarks derived from aggregate Google Ads performance data across hundreds of RevForge-managed trade contractor accounts in the US. CPL benchmarks are segmented by trade type and metro market size (small <200k, medium 200k–1M, large 1M+).

Budget recommendation = your desired leads × CPL benchmark (±15% range reflects normal campaign variance). Booked jobs = leads × your close rate. Revenue = booked jobs × average job value. ROI = (revenue − budget) ÷ budget × 100.

Benchmarks are updated periodically. Actual CPLs depend on ad copy quality, landing page conversion rate, bid strategy, Quality Score, and local competitive density. Use this as a planning baseline, not a guarantee.
