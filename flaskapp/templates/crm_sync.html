What gets synced

Read-only access. Your job data stays in your CRM — RevForge only reads what's needed to close the attribution loop.

Pulled from your CRM

  • New job requests with source (phone, web form, referral)
  • Job status changes (scheduled → dispatched → completed)
  • Invoice amounts and service types
  • Cancellations and no-shows
  • Technician capacity and schedule availability
  • Customer repeat / new status

What RevForge does with it

  • Match booked jobs to ad clicks via phone number + timestamp
  • Calculate true cost per booked job per campaign
  • Auto-reduce bids when your schedule hits capacity
  • Surface highest-revenue service types by channel
  • Alert when cancellation rate spikes by lead source
  • Build retargeting audiences from completed customer list
