How to read this

Impression Share (IS): How often their ads show vs. all eligible auctions. Higher = more dominant.
Overlap Rate: How often you both showed ads in the same auction. High overlap = direct competition.
Above You: When you both showed, how often their ad appeared above yours.
You Outrank: When you both showed, how often your ad appeared above theirs — or they didn't show at all.
