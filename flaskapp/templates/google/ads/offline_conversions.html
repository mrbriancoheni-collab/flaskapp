Why This Matters

Without call tracking, Google thinks your ads aren't working — so it stops showing them. This fixes that. Every qualified phone call gets sent back to Google as a conversion, giving the AI the signal it needs to keep your ads in front of the right customers.

{# Automatic / CallRail tab #}

Set up CallRail to send calls automatically

Follow these steps in your CallRail account to start tracking calls.

{# Step 1 #}
1

Copy your unique webhook URL

This URL is specific to your account — it's where CallRail will send call data.

{# Step 2 #}
2

In CallRail: go to Settings → Notifications

Click Add Webhook and paste the URL above. Select the Call event type.

{# Step 3 #}
3

Enable Google Ads tracking in CallRail

In CallRail go to Settings → Integrations → Google Ads. Enable the integration so CallRail captures the gclid from each Google Ads click.

{# Step 4 #}
4

Sync your Google Ads Conversion Actions

Make sure you have a conversion action named "Phone Call Lead" in Google Ads. Sync conversion actions →

{# Optional secret #}
Optional: Set the CALLRAIL_WEBHOOK_SECRET environment variable to the CallRail webhook signing secret for request validation.

Upload conversions manually

    "conversion_time": "2024-01-15 14:30:00",
    "value": 0,
    "conversion_name": "Phone Call Lead"
  }
]
