How Multi-Agent AI Works

Unlike single-AI tools, our multi-agent system mirrors how expert PPC teams work: strategic planning, daily operations, and tactical execution—all working in harmony.

🎯

Strategic Layer

Like your CMO, the Strategic Director sets goals, allocates budgets, and identifies scaling opportunities based on business objectives.

⚙️

Campaign Manager, Budget Guardian, and Quality Score Agent handle daily management, prevent overspend, and optimize for relevance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How are agents different from regular AI tools?

Traditional tools use a single AI to analyze everything. Our multi-agent system mirrors how expert PPC teams work: specialists for strategy, operations, and tactics working together. Each agent has specific expertise and they coordinate via an event bus.

What does "95% auto-executed" mean?

Low-risk, high-confidence decisions (like blocking "free plumber" searches) happen automatically. High-risk decisions (like pausing campaigns or major budget changes) require your approval. You stay in control of big decisions while agents handle the tedious daily work.

Can I customize agent behavior?

Yes! Via the Admin → AI Agents panel, you can: adjust auto-execute thresholds, add custom business prompts (e.g., "seasonal HVAC company"), override risk levels, and create IF/THEN business rules for each agent.

Which agents run most frequently?

Negative Keyword Agent and Keyword Optimizer run daily. Ad Copy Agent runs weekly. Strategic Director runs weekly for strategic reviews. Budget Guardian monitors 24/7 for emergency situations. You can adjust frequencies in the admin panel.
