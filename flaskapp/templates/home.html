Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know

Get answers to common questions about FieldSprout

What does FieldSprout actually do?
FieldSprout uses autonomous AI agents to optimize your Google Ads campaigns 24/7. It analyzes search terms, budgets, bidding, and performance patterns—then makes the same adjustments an expert would make, but faster and continuously.
How does FieldSprout reduce my cost per lead?
The platform reduces wasted spend by tightening keyword targeting, removing low-intent traffic, adjusting bids, and reallocating budget toward high-converting opportunities. Most home service accounts see a 20–40% CPL reduction within 30 days.
How is FieldSprout different from an agency?
Agencies rely on humans with limited time. FieldSprout uses autonomous AI agents that optimize continuously, delivering better performance at lower cost. You pay for outcomes, not hours—and you keep full control of your Google Ads account.
Does FieldSprout work for home services?
Yes. FieldSprout was built specifically for home service industries such as pest control, HVAC, plumbing, roofing, electrical, lawn care, solar, restoration, and garage door repair.
Will FieldSprout replace my agency?
FieldSprout can replace your agency or augment it by delivering continuous AI-driven optimization. Many companies use FieldSprout to replace expensive retainers while improving performance.
Does FieldSprout create ads automatically?
Yes. FieldSprout's Ad Copy Agent generates and tests new headlines, descriptions, assets, callouts, sitelinks automatically—optimizing for the best performing variations.
What makes FieldSprout better than Adalysis or Optmyzr?
Those tools rely on rules, checklists, and manual workflows. FieldSprout uses autonomous AI agents that learn every day, adjust campaigns in real time, run multi-step optimizations, and self-diagnose issues. It's not a rules engine—it's a performance engine.
Do I still have full control of my Google Ads account?
Yes. We connect to your account via Google's API but never take ownership. You can override any change at any time and retain complete control over your account.
How long does it take to see results?
Most accounts see improvement within 7–14 days and significant gains within 30–45 days as the AI agents learn your account patterns and optimize performance.
Is FieldSprout safe and compliant?
Yes. Everything is fully compliant with Google Ads Terms and API policies. All optimization runs inside your account through official channels with complete transparency.
