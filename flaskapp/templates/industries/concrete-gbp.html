Google Business Profile for Concrete Contractors

Concrete project transformations are among the most visually dramatic in home services — a crumbling driveway replaced with smooth new concrete, a bare backyard transformed by a stamped patio. Your GBP should showcase this visual impact with before/after photos that sell the project before a homeowner ever calls.

Concrete GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Concrete Contractor
  • • Secondary: Paving Contractor + Stamped Concrete Contractor
  • • Photos: Before/after by project type — driveways, patios, stamped concrete
  • • Seasonal: Spring content in February; pause marketing below 40°F
  • • Services: Separate listings for each project type

Concrete GBP Services: One Entry Per Project Type

Each project type attracts different buyers with different motivations. List them individually:

  • Driveway Installation — "New concrete driveway installation. Smooth, broom, or exposed aggregate finish."
  • Patio Installation — "Concrete patio installation. Broom finish, exposed aggregate, and stamped options."
  • Stamped Concrete — "Decorative stamped concrete for patios, walkways, and pool decks."
  • Concrete Repair — "Concrete crack repair, resurfacing, and restoration."
  • Sidewalk Installation — "Residential and commercial sidewalk installation and repair."
  • Foundation Work — "Concrete foundations for new construction and additions."

Before/After Photos: The Concrete GBP Strategy

Concrete before/after photos are conversion drivers — they show the dramatic transformation and help homeowners visualize their own project:

  • Cracked, stained, or heaving driveway vs. smooth new concrete pour
  • Bare backyard vs. finished stamped concrete patio with furniture set up
  • Old weathered concrete vs. freshly resurfaced and sealed
  • Organize photos by project type so browsers can find relevant examples quickly

Seasonal GBP Posting for Concrete

Concrete work has hard temperature limits. Align your GBP posting with the season:

  • February: "Now scheduling spring 2025 concrete projects. Limited slots available — book now."
  • March-May: Spring project photos — driveways and patios as they're completed
  • June-August: Active season — weekly project photos, showing range of project types
  • September: "Get your project done before the cold. Fall slots still available."
  • October: Last projects before season close — "see you next spring"

Stamped Concrete GBP Content: The High-Value Differentiator

Stamped concrete generates higher job values and more pride-of-ownership photos. Lean into it:

  • Post stamped concrete photos organized by pattern (ashlar slate, cobblestone, wood plank) and color
  • Show completed outdoor living spaces: stamped patio + furniture + fire pit = aspirational content
  • Customers share photos of their stamped patios — ask permission to repost on GBP

Concrete GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my concrete contractor Google Business Profile?

Add Stamped Concrete Contractor as a secondary category, list each project type as an individual service, upload before/after photos organized by project type, publish spring scheduling posts in February, and text review requests within 48 hours of completing driveways and patios.

What GBP categories should a concrete contractor use?

Primary: Concrete Contractor. Secondary: Paving Contractor, Stamped Concrete Contractor. Stamped Concrete Contractor captures the highest-value decorative concrete searches. Paving Contractor captures driveway searches alongside concrete.

How do I get more concrete Google reviews?

Within 48 hours of project completion, with stamped concrete customers especially likely to share. Ask customers to describe the project type — "they poured our stamped patio" adds keyword relevance. Target 4-6 per month during active season.

What should concrete contractors post on Google Business Profile?

Before/after of driveway and patio projects, stamped concrete pattern and color variety, spring scheduling posts in February, fall urgency posts in September, and weekly project photos during active season. Before/after pairs are the highest-converting concrete GBP content.
