🏗️ Concrete Lead Generation

Get More Concrete Leads

Concrete contracting is a visual, seasonal, high-ticket business — your leads need to see your work before they call. Here's how to generate concrete leads that turn into $3,000-8,000 residential projects and $10,000-50,000 commercial jobs.

Concrete Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $70–$140 (residential) / $100–$150 (commercial)
  • • Avg residential project: $3,000–$8,000
  • • Commercial slab avg: $10,000–$50,000+
  • • No pours below: 40°F (seasonal pause in northern markets)
  • • Best visual content: Before/after driveways and stamped patios

Seasonal Concrete Marketing: Maximize Your Pouring Season

Concrete contractors in northern markets have a 7-8 month pouring season (April-November). Your marketing should reflect this:

  • Pre-season (February-March): Start ads early to capture planning-phase homeowners. Many schedule concrete projects 4-8 weeks in advance.
  • Spring surge (April-May): Peak residential season. Maximum budgets on driveway and patio campaigns.
  • Summer (June-August): Commercial work peaks. Target business owners and property managers for parking lots and commercial slabs.
  • Fall close (September-October): Last chance for residential before temperatures drop. "Last chance before winter" urgency messaging converts well.
  • Winter (November-March): Pause ads in freezing climates. Use this time for website improvements and review gathering.

Project-Specific Campaign Structure

Different concrete projects attract different customers — don't mix them in one campaign:

  • Driveway Replacement: "driveway replacement cost," "concrete driveway installer near me" — homeowners, $3,000-6,000 avg
  • Patios & Stamped Concrete: "concrete patio installation," "stamped concrete patio" — homeowners wanting upgrades, $2,000-8,000 avg
  • Foundations & Slabs: "concrete slab contractor," "foundation pouring" — builders, commercial, higher ticket
  • Commercial: "commercial concrete contractor," "parking lot concrete" — businesses, $10,000-100,000+

Visual Marketing for Concrete

Concrete is a visual purchase — homeowners want to see finished work before calling. Invest in:

  • Before/after driveway photos (most requested from prospects)
  • Stamped concrete close-up photos (premium look, higher ticket)
  • Short project walkthrough videos for Meta Ads
  • Seasonal "now booking" posts on GBP and Meta
