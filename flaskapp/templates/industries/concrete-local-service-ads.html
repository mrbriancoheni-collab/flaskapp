Why Local Service Ads Are Critical for Concrete Companies

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) appear at the very top of search results—above regular Google Ads and organic listings. When someone searches "concrete driveway installer near me" or "stamped concrete [city]", LSAs are the first thing they see. And unlike Google Ads where you pay per click, LSAs charge per lead (actual phone call or message), making them extremely cost-effective.

The Google Guaranteed badge that comes with LSAs provides instant trust—critical for concrete projects where homeowners are making $4,000-20,000 investment decisions. Google has verified your business license, insurance, and background checks, giving nervous homeowners confidence to call you first.

The Concrete LSA Challenge: Visual Trust and Competition

LSAs show only 2-3 concrete contractors at the top of search results. If you're not in that top group, you're invisible. LSA ranking for concrete contractors is especially competitive because project values are high. Success depends on:

  • Reviews with project photos — visual proof is critical for concrete (homeowners want to see your work)
  • High review count — 25+ reviews needed to compete effectively
  • Fast response times — responding to leads within 2 minutes improves ranking dramatically
  • Google Guaranteed status — required for LSAs, increases trust for large projects
  • Accurate service categories — appearing for the right project types (driveway vs. patio vs. stamped)

Concrete LSA Best Practices

Profile Optimization Checklist

  • ✅ Complete Google Guaranteed verification (license, insurance, background checks)
  • ✅ 25+ reviews with 4.5+ star average (ideally 50%+ with project photos)
  • ✅ Accurate business hours (update for seasonal changes)
  • ✅ Professional project photos (completed driveways, patios, stamped work)
  • ✅ Detailed service area (ZIP codes within profitable drive distance)
  • ✅ Relevant service categories (driveways, patios, stamped, repair)
  • ✅ Appropriate weekly budget (adjusted seasonally for demand and capacity)

Lead Response Protocol

  1. Respond within 2 minutes — speed is critical for LSA ranking and booking rate
  2. Call back via phone — even if lead came via message, call them (higher booking rate)
  3. Qualify the project — location, project type (driveway/patio/stamped), timeline, budget
  4. Schedule site visit — give specific appointment time within 2-3 days if possible
  5. Send confirmation — text or email with estimator name, arrival time, contact info

Review Generation Best Practices

  • Request review 1-2 days after project completion (give concrete time to cure, customer to enjoy it)
  • Use SMS with direct Google review link (easier than email)
  • Ask for photos: "Would you mind adding a photo of your new driveway to your review?"
  • Don't incentivize reviews (violates Google policies)
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours with project-specific details

Common Concrete LSA Questions

How much do concrete LSA leads cost?

Driveway/patio leads: $30-70 per lead (varies by market competitiveness)
Stamped/decorative leads: $70-140 per lead (higher project value = higher cost)
Repair leads: $20-45 per lead (lower project value)

Unlike Google Ads (pay per click), LSAs charge per actual lead—a phone call lasting 30+ seconds or a message conversation. This makes LSAs more cost-effective for concrete contractors because you're not paying for clicks from price shoppers.

Can I run LSAs and Google Ads simultaneously?

Yes, and you should. LSAs and Google Ads work together:

  • LSAs: High-intent local searches, pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed badge builds trust
  • Google Ads: Broader reach, specific project type campaigns (stamped concrete, decorative), control over messaging

Most successful concrete contractors run both. LSAs capture the highest-intent local traffic, while Google Ads expand reach for premium projects and broader targeting.

How long does Google Guaranteed verification take?

Typical timeline: 5-10 days after submitting all required documents. The process includes:

  • Contractor license verification (2-3 days)
  • Insurance certificate review (1-2 days)
  • Background checks (3-7 days, sometimes instant)

Delays happen if licenses are expired, insurance doesn't meet minimums ($1M liability typically required), or documents don't match business name exactly.

What if I get a bad review on my LSA profile?

Respond professionally within 24 hours. For concrete projects, address the specific issue (cracking, color variation, timeline delays) and explain your resolution or perspective. A thoughtful response to a negative review can actually build trust.

Don't: Get defensive or argue. This makes you look unprofessional.

Do: Generate 8-12 new positive reviews with project photos to dilute the impact. One 2-star review among 60 five-star reviews with photos has minimal impact.
