Concrete Marketing FAQs

How do concrete contractors get more leads online?

The three highest-ROI channels for concrete contractors are Google Ads, Google Business Profile, and Local Services Ads. Concrete has less emergency urgency than plumbing, so lead nurturing and fast follow-up within 5 minutes are critical to converting inquiries into estimates.

What's a good cost per lead for concrete work?

Industry benchmark for concrete leads is $70-140. With optimized Google Ads and a strong GBP, RevForge-managed accounts typically achieve $75-95 per lead. Since average concrete jobs run $3,000-8,000 residential and $10,000+ commercial, even $140 CPL generates strong ROI.

When should concrete companies increase their marketing budget?

Spring (March-May) and early fall (September-October) are peak seasons. Avoid heavy spending in winter in northern climates — concrete can't be poured below 40°F. RevForge automatically adjusts budgets to match seasonal demand patterns and local weather.

How do concrete contractors rank higher on Google Maps?

Ranking for "concrete contractor near me" requires consistent review generation (5+ reviews/month), fully optimized GBP with project photos, accurate service areas, and citations in Houzz, Angi, and HomeAdvisor. RevForge automates review requests after every completed job.
