Google Business Profile for Electricians

Electrical work requires more trust than any other trade. Your GBP needs to communicate licensing, insurance, and permit compliance upfront. Here's how to optimize your electrical GBP to build trust, rank for high-value services like panel upgrades and EV chargers, and generate the reviews that close jobs.

Electrical GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Electrician
  • • Secondary: Electrical Installation Service + Emergency Electrician + EV Charger Installer
  • • Description: Include state license number in first paragraph
  • • Services: Panel upgrade, EV charger, generator, rewiring, outlet installation
  • • Reviews: Ask customers to mention licensing and permit compliance

Trust Signals in Electrical GBP: Non-Negotiable Elements

Homeowners are more cautious about hiring electricians than most other trades. Your GBP must answer their primary trust questions before they even visit your website:

  • License number in description: "Licensed Electrical Contractor — License #[XXXXX]. Serving [City] since [year]."
  • Insurance mentioned: "Fully licensed and insured. We pull all permits and schedule inspections."
  • Permit compliance: Homeowners who've had bad experiences with unlicensed work specifically search for "licensed electrician" and "permitted electrical work"
  • BBB accreditation badge: If you have BBB accreditation, display it in your profile photos

EV Charger GBP Category: The Underutilized Opportunity

EV charger installation searches are growing 50-80% annually. Most electricians haven't added the EV charger category to their GBP:

  • Add "Electric Vehicle Charging Station Installer" as a GBP secondary category
  • Add EV charger installation as a service with a description: "Level 2 EV charger installation for all major brands (Tesla, ChargePoint, Wallbox, Lectron)"
  • Upload photos of every EV charger installation — these photos build credibility for the growing EV owner audience
  • Early positioning in this category provides first-mover advantage in your market

Panel Upgrade GBP Content

Panel upgrades ($3,000-8,000) are the highest-value residential electrical job. Your GBP should speak to panel upgrade buyers:

  • Service: "200-Amp Panel Upgrade — permits included, inspection scheduled"
  • Post type: "Panel upgrade complete — 100A upgraded to 200A, ready for EV charger and new circuits"
  • Before/after panel photos show the transformation from old/crowded to new/organized
  • Review ask: "If you're happy with the panel upgrade, a quick Google review mentioning the work really helps us"

Electrical GBP Posts: Building Authority Around Safety and Quality

  • GFCI outlet installation posts — "Protect your family with GFCI outlets in kitchens and bathrooms"
  • Surge protection posts in spring — storm season electrical protection
  • Generator installation posts before hurricane/storm season in your region
  • Holiday lighting installation in November — residential upsell opportunity
  • "We pull permits" posts — proactive trust-building for the homeowner who's been burned before

Electrical GBP FAQs

Add EV Charger Installer as a secondary category, include your state license number in the description, list panel upgrades and EV charger installation as services, upload photos of completed panel and EV work, and ask customers to mention licensing and permits in reviews.

What GBP categories should an electrician use?

Primary: Electrician. Secondary: Electrical Installation Service, Emergency Electrician, Electric Vehicle Charging Station Installer, Generator Installation Service. EV Charger Installer is the most underutilized high-growth category for electricians right now.

How do I get more electrical contractor Google reviews?

Ask after every panel upgrade and EV charger install — customers are excited about high-value completed work. Request that customers mention licensing and permit compliance in reviews — this builds keyword relevance and trust signals. Text same-day for highest response rates.

What should electricians post on Google Business Profile?

Completed panel upgrades before/after, EV charger installations, generator installs pre-storm season, GFCI and surge protection safety tips, and "we pull permits" trust-building posts. Holiday lighting installation in November is a strong seasonal upsell opportunity.
