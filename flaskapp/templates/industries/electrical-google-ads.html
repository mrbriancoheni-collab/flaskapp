Why Electrical Google Ads Are Different

Electrical contractors face unique Google Ads challenges that generic marketing agencies don't understand. Your customers search differently depending on whether they have a dangerous emergency (sparking outlet, burning smell), a planned upgrade (panel replacement, EV charger), or routine work (install ceiling fan, add outlets). Your margins vary dramatically between a $150 service call and a $3,500 panel upgrade.

Generic Google Ads strategies fail because they don't account for these electrical-specific realities. You need campaigns that separate true emergencies from scheduled work. You need aggressive negative keyword strategies to block the DIY crowd looking for YouTube tutorials. And you need smart geographic targeting to avoid unprofitable long-distance service calls.

The Electrical Google Ads Problem: Wasted Spend on DIY Traffic

Most electrical contractors overspend on Google Ads by attracting DIY searchers, people shopping for parts, and job seekers instead of customers ready to hire a licensed electrician. They run generic "electrical services" campaigns that waste 40-60% of ad spend on clicks that will never convert.

The most common electrical Google Ads mistakes:

  • Mixing emergency and scheduled work in the same campaigns with same bids
  • Broad match keywords like "electrical" that attract DIY searchers
  • No negative keywords for "DIY", "YouTube", "parts", "jobs", "how to"
  • Generic ad copy that doesn't speak to safety concerns or urgency
  • No service area targeting leading to unprofitable distant calls
  • Not separating residential from commercial (very different audiences)

Common Electrical Google Ads Questions

Should I advertise residential and commercial electrical separately?

Yes. Commercial and residential electrical work are completely different businesses with different keywords, margins, decision-makers, and sales cycles. If you serve both, create separate campaigns. If you're residential-only (most small electrical contractors), add "commercial" as a negative keyword.

How much should electrical contractors spend on Google Ads?

A typical rule of thumb: $40-80 per lead target for emergency/repair work, $80-150 per lead for panel upgrades and EV chargers. If your average panel upgrade generates $2,500 in revenue at 40% margin, you can afford $80-120 per lead and still be profitable.

Most electrical contractors spend $2,000-5,000 per month on Google Ads, with some markets requiring more due to competition.

What's a good conversion rate for electrical Google Ads?

Emergency repair campaigns: 8-15% conversion rate (form fill or call)
Panel upgrade campaigns: 5-10% conversion rate
EV charger/generator campaigns: 4-8% conversion rate

If you're below these benchmarks, you likely have issues with:

  • Irrelevant traffic (poor keyword targeting or missing negative keywords)
  • Weak landing pages (slow load time, poor mobile experience, unclear CTA)
  • Misaligned ad copy (promise doesn't match landing page)

Should electrical contractors use Google Local Services Ads (LSAs)?

Absolutely. Google Local Services Ads (the "Google Guaranteed" listings) are extremely effective for electricians. They appear above regular Google Ads and you only pay per lead, not per click. LSAs work especially well for emergency electrical repairs and general electrical work.

FieldSprout manages both Google Ads and Google Local Services Ads in a coordinated strategy. LSAs handle branded and emergency searches, while Google Ads expand reach for specific services like panel upgrades and EV charger installation.
