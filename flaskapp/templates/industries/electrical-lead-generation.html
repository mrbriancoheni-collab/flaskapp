⚡ Electrical Lead Generation

Get More Electrical Leads

Electrical contractors have an unusual marketing challenge: general service calls (outlets, switches, repairs) are low-ticket, while panel upgrades, EV charger installations, and commercial work are high-margin. Here's how to build a lead pipeline that prioritizes the jobs worth your license.

Electrical Lead Generation Benchmarks

  • • Google Ads CPL: $80–$140 (panel/EV charger on higher end)
  • • EV charger avg ticket: $1,200–$2,500
  • • Panel upgrade avg ticket: $3,000–$8,000
  • • Commercial CPL: $150–$250 (2x residential but larger jobs)
  • • Top negative keywords: "electrician school," "DIY wiring," "electrical jobs"

Prioritize High-Margin Electrical Jobs in Your Marketing

The most important distinction in electrical marketing: not all leads are equal. A $150 outlet repair and a $6,000 panel upgrade are both "electrical leads" — but they require completely different campaigns, budgets, and messaging. Mixing them hurts both: your panel upgrade campaigns become diluted with low-ticket repair traffic, and your repair campaigns overpay for the wrong searchers.

The 4 Channels That Drive Electrical Leads

1. Google Ads: Segmented by Job Type and Margin

Run separate campaigns for each high-value job type:

  • Panel Upgrades: "electrical panel replacement," "200 amp upgrade," "panel upgrade cost" — high bids, financing messaging
  • EV Charger Installation: "EV charger installation near me," "level 2 charger installation," "home EV charger cost" — target EV vehicle owners
  • Emergency Electrical: "electrician near me," "electrical emergency," "power outage repair" — 24/7, response time messaging
  • General Service: "outlet repair," "ceiling fan installation," "light switch replacement" — lower bids, efficient scheduling

Negative keywords: "electrician jobs," "electrician school," "DIY electrical," "DIY wiring," "electrical code lookup," "electrical supply"

2. EV Charger Meta Ads (High-Growth Opportunity)

The EV market is growing 30%+ annually — and every new EV owner needs a Level 2 home charger installed. Meta Ads can target people who recently purchased a vehicle or have shown interest in EVs. These leads are high-intent and high-ticket ($1,200-2,500 per install). This is the fastest-growing lead source for forward-thinking electrical contractors.

3. Google LSAs for Trust-Sensitive Electrical Work

Homeowners are more cautious about electrical work than almost any other trade — fire risk, code compliance, and safety concerns make trust critical. The Google Guaranteed badge on LSAs provides pre-validated trust that significantly improves call rates for electrical services. Electrical LSA CPL: $60-110.

4. Commercial Electrical Referral Networks

Commercial electrical work pays 2x residential CPL but requires different acquisition channels — general contractors, property managers, facility managers, and tenant improvement contractors. Strategies: LinkedIn outreach, NECA membership, AGC networking, and a formal referral program for GCs who use you on residential projects.

Electrical Lead Generation FAQs

How do I get more electrician leads?

Google Ads for high-value searches (panel upgrades, EV chargers), Google LSAs for trust badge, GBP for local Maps visibility, and Meta Ads targeting EV vehicle owners. Separate high-margin jobs from general service calls for better ROI.

What's the best way to get electrical customers?

Target high-margin jobs specifically: "electrical panel replacement," "EV charger installation," "200 amp upgrade." Panel upgrades average $3,000-8,000 and EV charger installs average $1,200-2,500 — worth the $80-140 CPL. Commercial work pays 2x but requires contractor referral networks.

How much do electrical leads cost?

Google Ads: $80-140. LSAs: $60-110. Meta EV charger campaigns: $70-120. Commercial leads: $150-250 but produce much larger projects. Residential service calls: $60-90 with $300-500 tickets.

What are the best electrical lead generation tips?

Separate high-value campaigns (panels, EV chargers) from general service. Target EV vehicle owners via Meta Ads. Show "Licensed Master Electrician" in all ads. Negative keywords: "electrician school," "DIY wiring," "electrical jobs." Build commercial referral programs with general contractors.
