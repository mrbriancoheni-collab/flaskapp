Get More Fencing Leads

Fencing is a planned purchase with a 2-4 week decision window — homeowners collect 3+ quotes before choosing. Here's how to win more fencing jobs by staying visible throughout the decision window and targeting the high-converting HOA and material-specific niches.

Fencing Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $60–$120 (material-specific on higher end)
  • • Avg residential job: $2,500–$7,000
  • • Decision window: 2–4 weeks (retargeting critical)
  • • HOA niche: Higher CPL but better close rates
  • • Best visual content: Before/after fence installations by material type

Material-Specific Lead Generation: The Fencing Advantage

Most fencing contractors run generic "fence installation" campaigns. The contractors who dominate their markets run separate campaigns by material — wood, vinyl, aluminum, chain link. Here's why it matters:

  • A homeowner searching "vinyl fence installation cost" is 80% likely to want vinyl — showing them a wood fence ad is irrelevant
  • Each material has different price points, different buyer motivations, and different competitive landscapes
  • Material-specific landing pages convert 2-3x better than generic "we install all fence types" pages

The HOA Fencing Niche

HOA communities are a high-value fencing niche. Homeowners in HOAs:

  • Need HOA-approved materials (usually vinyl or aluminum — no wood or chain link)
  • Are less price-sensitive because material is predetermined
  • Generate neighbor referrals when they install — seeing a new fence triggers inquiry from 2-3 neighbors

Target HOA communities with keywords like "HOA approved fence contractor" and "vinyl fence HOA compliant." Meta Ads let you target homeowners in specific neighborhoods — identify the HOA communities in your area and target them directly.

The 2-4 Week Decision Window Strategy

Fencing decisions take 2-4 weeks. During this time, homeowners are comparing quotes from 3+ contractors. Stay visible with:

  • Retargeting ads on Meta to website visitors for 30 days
  • Email follow-up sequence after estimate (Day 3, Day 7, Day 14)
  • Text follow-up at Day 5 with a "your estimate is still available" message
