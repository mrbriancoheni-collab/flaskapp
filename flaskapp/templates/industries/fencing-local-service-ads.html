Why Local Service Ads Are Critical for Fencing Companies

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) appear at the very top of search results—above regular Google Ads and organic listings. When someone searches "fence installer near me" or "privacy fence [city]", LSAs are the first thing they see. And unlike Google Ads where you pay per click, LSAs charge per lead (actual phone call or message), making them extremely cost-effective.

The Google Guaranteed badge that comes with LSAs provides instant trust—critical for fencing projects where homeowners are making $3,000-8,000 investment decisions. Google has verified your business license, insurance, and background checks, giving homeowners confidence to call you first.

The Fencing LSA Challenge: Visual Proof and Fast Response

LSAs show only 2-3 fencing contractors at the top of search results. If you're not in that top group, you're invisible. LSA ranking for fencing contractors depends heavily on visual proof (fence photos in reviews) and response speed. Success factors:

  • Reviews with fence photos — visual proof is critical (homeowners want to see your work quality and styles)
  • High review count — 20+ reviews needed to compete effectively in most markets
  • Fast response times — responding to leads within 2 minutes improves ranking dramatically
  • Google Guaranteed status — required for LSAs, increases trust
  • Accurate service categories — appearing for the right fence types (privacy, chain link, ornamental)

Fencing LSA Best Practices

Profile Optimization Checklist

  • ✅ Complete Google Guaranteed verification (license, insurance, background checks)
  • ✅ 20+ reviews with 4.5+ star average (ideally 50%+ with fence photos)
  • ✅ Accurate business hours (update for seasonal changes)
  • ✅ Professional fence photos (completed privacy fences, ornamental, chain link)
  • ✅ Detailed service area (ZIP codes within profitable drive distance)
  • ✅ Relevant service categories (privacy, chain link, vinyl, repair)
  • ✅ Appropriate weekly budget (adjusted seasonally for demand and capacity)

Lead Response Protocol

  1. Respond within 2 minutes — speed is critical for LSA ranking and booking rate
  2. Call back via phone — even if lead came via message, call them (higher booking rate)
  3. Qualify the project — location, fence type (privacy/chain link/ornamental), linear feet, timeline
  4. Schedule site visit — give specific appointment time within 2-3 days if possible
  5. Send confirmation — text or email with estimator name, arrival time, contact info

Review Generation Best Practices

  • Request review 1-2 days after project completion (let customer enjoy their new fence first)
  • Use SMS with direct Google review link (easier than email)
  • Ask for photos: "Would you mind adding a photo of your new fence to your review?"
  • Don't incentivize reviews (violates Google policies)
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours with project-specific details (fence type, linear feet)

Common Fencing LSA Questions

How much do fencing LSA leads cost?

Privacy fence leads: $25-60 per lead (varies by market competitiveness)
Ornamental/decorative leads: $50-100 per lead (higher project value = higher cost)
Chain link leads: $20-45 per lead (lower project value)
Repair leads: $15-35 per lead

Unlike Google Ads (pay per click), LSAs charge per actual lead—a phone call lasting 30+ seconds or a message conversation. This makes LSAs more cost-effective because you're not paying for clicks from DIY materials shoppers.

Can I run LSAs and Google Ads simultaneously?

Yes, and you should. LSAs and Google Ads work together:

  • LSAs: High-intent local searches, pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed badge builds trust
  • Google Ads: Broader reach, specific fence type campaigns (ornamental, custom), control over messaging

Most successful fencing contractors run both. LSAs capture the highest-intent local traffic, while Google Ads expand reach for specialty fences and broader targeting.

How long does Google Guaranteed verification take?

Typical timeline: 5-10 days after submitting all required documents. The process includes:

  • Contractor license verification (2-3 days)
  • Insurance certificate review (1-2 days)
  • Background checks (3-7 days, sometimes instant)

Delays happen if licenses are expired, insurance doesn't meet minimums, or documents don't match business name exactly.

What if I get a bad review on my LSA profile?

Respond professionally within 24 hours. For fencing projects, address the specific issue (quality concern, timeline delay, material mismatch) and explain your resolution or perspective. A thoughtful response builds trust.

Don't: Get defensive or argue. This makes you look unprofessional.

Do: Generate 8-12 new positive reviews with fence photos to dilute the impact. One 2-star review among 50 five-star reviews with photos has minimal impact.
