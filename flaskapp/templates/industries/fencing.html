Fencing Marketing FAQs

How do fencing companies get more customers online?

Fencing leads come primarily from Google Ads, Google Business Profile, and Meta Ads showcasing completed work. Unlike emergency services, fencing is a planned purchase — being visible across multiple channels during the 2-4 week research phase is critical to winning the job.

What's the average cost per lead for fencing contractors?

Fencing CPL benchmarks range from $60-120 depending on competition and job types. Premium materials attract slightly higher CPLs but higher ticket sizes. RevForge helps you target the job types with the best margin for your crew.

How important are reviews for fencing company rankings?

Reviews are the #1 ranking factor for the Google Maps 3-pack. A fencing company with 50+ reviews and 4.7+ stars will outrank competitors with fewer reviews, even with a weaker website. Aim for 4-5 new reviews per month — RevForge automates this via text after every job.

Should fencing contractors target HOA-related keywords?

Yes — HOA-compliant fencing is a significant niche. Searches like "HOA approved fence contractor" and "vinyl fence HOA approved" convert at a high rate because the homeowner has already decided on the material. RevForge includes HOA-intent keywords automatically.
