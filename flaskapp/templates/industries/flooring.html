Flooring seasonal intelligence built in

Flooring demand is tightly linked to home sales and spring renovation timing. RevForge automatically ramps spring campaigns before the season peaks — and shifts to refinishing and replacement keywords when installation demand softens.

  • Spring installation surge — campaigns ramped in February for March–May peak
  • Move season (May–August) — targets buyers in home sale/purchase process
  • Refinishing campaigns year-round — hardwood refinishing demand is less seasonal
  • Fall basement/remodel campaigns — LVP and carpet replacement peak in September
