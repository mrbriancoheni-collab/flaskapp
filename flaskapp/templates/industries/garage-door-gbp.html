Google Business Profile for Garage Door Companies

50% of garage door jobs are emergencies — broken springs, doors off track, cars trapped inside. Your GBP needs to communicate same-day emergency availability while also showcasing the door styles and opener brands that drive planned replacement purchases.

Garage Door GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Garage Door Supplier
  • • Secondary: Garage Door Repair Service + Garage Door Installer
  • • Hours: Mark 24/7 if you handle emergency repairs after hours
  • • Description: "Same-Day Garage Door Repair — Springs, Openers, New Doors"
  • • Photos: Door styles + before/after emergency repairs

Emergency Garage Door GBP Settings

Getting the emergency settings right in your GBP captures the high-urgency calls that drive revenue:

  • Hours: If you take evening and weekend emergency calls, mark as "Open 24 Hours" or set accurate extended hours
  • Description: "Same-Day Garage Door Repair — Broken Springs, Off-Track Doors, Opener Failures. Emergency Service Available."
  • Attributes: Enable "Emergency Service" if available in your category
  • Services: List "Emergency Garage Door Repair" as an explicit service with response time

Garage Door Services Section: Complete Coverage

A complete services section ensures your GBP appears for the full range of garage door searches:

  • Broken Spring Replacement — "Torsion and extension spring replacement. Same-day service."
  • Garage Door Opener Installation — "LiftMaster, Chamberlain, Genie, and Craftsman opener installation."
  • New Garage Door Installation — "Steel, carriage house, and contemporary door styles. Free quotes."
  • Garage Door Cable Repair — "Broken cable repair and replacement."
  • Off-Track Door Repair — "Door off-track repair — same-day emergency service."
  • Smart Opener Upgrade — "WiFi-enabled opener upgrades. Control from your phone."

Door Style Photos: Converting the New Door Buyer

New garage door installations ($1,500-4,000) are a visual purchase. Homeowners want to see styles before deciding:

  • Organize your GBP photos by door style: steel, carriage house, contemporary, wood-look
  • Show the same home before and after — the curb appeal transformation is compelling
  • Include photos with different home styles — colonial, ranch, modern — so homeowners can find a match
  • Upload new door installation photos within 48 hours of every job

Post-Emergency Review Strategy

Emergency repair customers are the most grateful review sources in home services:

  • Text the review link within 30 minutes of completing a broken spring or emergency repair
  • The customer just had their car released from a trapped garage — they are maximally relieved
  • Reviews mentioning "same-day," "broken spring," "emergency" add keyword relevance that attracts more emergency searches

Garage Door GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my garage door Google Business Profile?

Mark 24/7 hours if you handle emergencies, lead your description with same-day emergency service, list broken spring/opener/cable/new door as separate services, upload door style photos by type, and text review requests within 30 minutes of completing emergency repairs.

What GBP categories should a garage door company use?

Primary: Garage Door Supplier. Secondary: Garage Door Repair Service, Garage Door Installer. All three together cover emergency repair, planned installation, and new door replacement searches.

How do I get more garage door Google reviews?

Text within 30 minutes of completing emergency repairs — customers with previously trapped cars are maximally grateful. New door installations are also strong review moments. Target 5-7 per month. Emergency-specific review language ("same-day broken spring") improves future emergency search visibility.

What should garage door companies post on Google Business Profile?

Door style before/after photos organized by type, "broken spring replaced same day" emergency capability posts, smart opener upgrade content, spring safety inspection reminders, and holiday curb appeal posts showing new door installations in November.
