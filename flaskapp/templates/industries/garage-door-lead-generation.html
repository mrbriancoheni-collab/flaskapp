🚪 Garage Door Lead Generation

Get More Garage Door Leads

Garage door is an emergency-heavy business — 50% of revenue comes from broken springs, failed openers, and doors that won't open. Here's how to build a garage door lead pipeline that captures emergency calls 24/7 and converts them into higher-ticket replacements.

Garage Door Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $45–$90 (emergency $45-65, new door $65-90)
  • • Emergency % of revenue: 50%
  • • Avg emergency repair ticket: $250–$450
  • • New door installation: $1,500–$3,500
  • • Spring peak: Door replacements surge in March–May

Emergency First: The Garage Door Lead Strategy

When a homeowner's garage door breaks with their car trapped inside, they search and call within minutes. This urgency-driven behavior makes garage door one of the highest-converting search categories — 20-30% of emergency searches result in a same-day booking. Your marketing must be visible at 7am on a Saturday just as much as Monday at noon.

The 4 Garage Door Lead Channels

1. Google Ads: Emergency + Installation

Separate campaigns for emergency repair vs. planned installation:

  • Emergency Repair: "garage door won't open," "broken garage door spring," "garage door stuck," "garage door repair near me" — 24/7, same-day service messaging
  • New Door Installation: "garage door installation," "new garage door cost," "garage door replacement" — spring focus, financing/style messaging
  • Opener Replacement: "garage door opener replacement," "smart garage door opener" — year-round, technology messaging

Negative keywords: "garage door parts," "DIY garage door spring," "garage door spring kit," "garage door jobs," "garage door panels wholesale"

2. Google Business Profile (The Emergency Caller's First Stop)

Most emergency garage door searches end with a GBP click — homeowners searching "garage door repair near me" click the first Maps result that has good reviews and is open. Critical GBP requirements:

  • 24/7 hours if you take emergency calls
  • "Emergency service available" in your description
  • Same-day service as a listed attribute
  • 5+ new reviews per month for consistent Maps visibility

3. The Emergency-to-Replacement Upsell Funnel

The most profitable garage door strategy: convert emergency repair customers into new door customers. After completing a repair ($350), every technician should offer a door replacement consultation. A door that needed a spring replacement probably needs replacement within 2-3 years anyway. Automated follow-up sequences ("Your garage door spring replacement was just $350 — a new door is $1,800 and comes with 5-year warranty") convert 15-20% of repair customers into replacement jobs within 90 days.
