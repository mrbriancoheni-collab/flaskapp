Google Business Profile for HVAC Companies

Your Google Business Profile is the first thing homeowners see when searching for HVAC service. 70% of emergency HVAC clicks go to a Maps result. Here's how to optimize your GBP to capture those clicks with the right categories, photos, posts, and reviews.

HVAC GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: HVAC Contractor
  • • Secondary categories: Air Conditioning Contractor + Heating Contractor + Furnace Repair Service
  • • Photos: 50+ (trucks, techs, equipment, completed installs)
  • • Reviews: 4.8+ stars, 50+ reviews for 3-pack; 5+ new/month
  • • Posts: Weekly seasonal content minimum

HVAC GBP Categories: Every Category Is a Search You Can Win

Google allows one primary and multiple secondary categories. Each category makes you eligible for a different set of local searches. For HVAC:

  • Primary: HVAC Contractor — captures all broad HVAC searches
  • Secondary: Air Conditioning Contractor — "AC company near me," "air conditioning repair"
  • Secondary: Heating Contractor — "heating company near me," "furnace service"
  • Secondary: Furnace Repair Service — "furnace repair near me," "furnace not working"
  • Secondary: Air Conditioning System Supplier — "new AC installation," "AC replacement"

HVAC GBP Services Section: The Individual Service Descriptions

The Services section lets you list each service individually with a name and description. Use it fully:

  • AC Repair — "Emergency and scheduled air conditioning repair. Same-day service available."
  • Furnace Repair — "Gas furnace, electric furnace, and heat pump repair. Licensed technicians."
  • AC Installation — "Central air conditioning installation for all home sizes. Financing available."
  • Furnace Installation — "New furnace installation and replacement. All major brands."
  • AC Tune-Up — "Annual AC maintenance and inspection to prevent breakdowns."
  • Maintenance Agreement — "Annual HVAC maintenance plans covering AC and heating systems."

HVAC GBP Photo Strategy

Profiles with 100+ photos receive significantly more views than profiles with fewer than 10. Build your photo library systematically:

  • Every tech takes a photo of each completed install — new unit outside, inside equipment, electrical connections
  • Team photos build trust — homeowners want to know who is coming to their home
  • Truck/van photos with company branding — recognized branding builds local familiarity
  • Before/after of equipment replacement — old rusted unit vs. new clean installation
  • Upload 2-3 photos per week to signal active business management to Google

HVAC GBP Seasonal Post Calendar

Weekly posts aligned with HVAC seasonal demand drive profile engagement and signal active management:

  • March-April: "AC tune-up special — $79 before summer. Limited spots available."
  • May-August: Same-day AC repair availability, "no cool air" emergency response
  • September-October: "Furnace inspection before heating season — book now"
  • November-February: "24/7 emergency heating repair — we answer every call"

HVAC Google Business Profile FAQs

How do I optimize my HVAC Google Business Profile?

Add all HVAC categories, fill out every service with individual descriptions, upload 50+ photos including trucks and completed installs, mark 24/7 hours if applicable, and generate 5+ new reviews per month. Post weekly with seasonal content — post activity influences local rankings.

What GBP categories should an HVAC company use?

Primary: HVAC Contractor. Secondary: Air Conditioning Contractor, Heating Contractor, Furnace Repair Service, Air Conditioning System Supplier. Each category captures a different set of local searches — don't leave any applicable category unclaimed.

How do I get more HVAC Google reviews?

Text a direct Google review link within 2 hours of every completed job. Target 5-10 new reviews per month. Respond to every review within 24 hours. Emergency repair customers are the most grateful immediately after a fix — perfect timing for a review ask. Never incentivize reviews.

What should HVAC companies post on Google Business Profile?

Spring: AC tune-up specials. Summer: same-day emergency repair availability. Fall: furnace inspection promotions. Winter: 24/7 emergency heating. Year-round: completed installs, team photos, and maintenance tips. Post at least weekly — Google weighs post frequency in local ranking signals.
