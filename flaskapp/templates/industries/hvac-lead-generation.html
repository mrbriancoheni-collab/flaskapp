The 4 Channels That Drive HVAC Leads

1. Google Ads (Highest-Intent Emergency Channel)

Google Ads capture HVAC leads at their peak urgency — someone searching "AC not working" at 2pm on a 95°F day will call within minutes. These are your highest-converting, highest-value leads. Key HVAC Google Ads tactics:

  • Separate emergency campaigns (AC repair, furnace repair) from maintenance and installation
  • Auto-scale budgets with seasonal patterns — peak summer AC, peak fall/winter heating
  • Use weather triggers to boost budgets when temperatures hit extremes
  • Aggressive negative keywords: "HVAC school," "DIY AC repair," "parts," "how to," "certification"

2. Google Local Services Ads (Google Guaranteed Badge)

LSAs appear above all Google Ads and organic results. For HVAC, the Google Guaranteed badge is a significant trust signal — homeowners calling in an AC emergency need to trust who they're calling. LSA CPL for HVAC runs $60-120 with pay-per-lead (no click waste). Priority requirements: 10+ reviews, license and insurance verification, and responsive lead handling.

3. Google Business Profile (Maps Visibility)

60% of local HVAC searches end with a Google Maps click — not a website visit. Your GBP listing is often the first place leads call from. To rank in the 3-pack for "HVAC near me" and "AC repair near me," you need:

  • 8-12 new reviews per month (velocity matters more than total count)
  • Complete service listings with pricing signals
  • Weekly GBP posts about seasonal services
  • Fast response to Q&A and reviews

4. Meta Ads (Maintenance and Seasonal Campaigns)

While Google captures emergency intent, Meta Ads create maintenance demand. "Schedule your spring AC tune-up before the summer rush" campaigns targeting homeowners in your area generate $50-90 CPL leads for maintenance work that isn't being driven by emergency. These campaigns are most effective January-April (pre-AC season) and August-October (pre-heating season).

HVAC Lead Follow-Up: The Critical 5 Minutes

HVAC is an emergency business. A homeowner who submits a form or calls and doesn't get an answer will call the next contractor in under 2 minutes. Every minute of follow-up delay costs you leads:

  • Under 5 minutes: 80%+ contact rate
  • 5-30 minutes: 50% contact rate
  • 30-60 minutes: 25% contact rate
  • Over 1 hour: Under 10% contact rate

Automated text responses acknowledging the lead and promising a call within X minutes buy you time if you're on a job. Set up instant text automation for every web form submission.

Converting HVAC Service Calls into Recurring Revenue

The highest-value HVAC customers are maintenance contract holders — $400-800/year of predictable, margin-rich revenue. Convert service call customers into maintenance contracts by:

  • Offering a discounted first tune-up as a maintenance plan entry point
  • Presenting the contract at the service call ("This repair is $320 today, or $99/month covers future repairs and tune-ups")
  • Sending automated maintenance reminders via email/text
  • Running annual renewal campaigns in spring and fall
