Local SEO for HVAC Companies

70% of emergency HVAC searches click a Google Maps result — not a website. Local SEO puts your business in the 3-pack when homeowners search "AC repair near me" or "furnace company [city]." Here's how to build a dominant local presence that generates free leads year-round.

Google Business Profile: The Core of HVAC Local SEO

Your GBP is the single most important local SEO asset for HVAC. The contractors who dominate the Maps 3-pack share common traits:

  • Complete category selection: Primary — "HVAC Contractor." Secondary — "Air Conditioning Contractor," "Heating Contractor," "Furnace Repair Service"
  • Services listed individually: AC Repair, Furnace Repair, AC Installation, Furnace Installation, Heat Pump Service — each with a unique description
  • Photos updated weekly: Trucks in service, technicians at work, before/after of equipment, completed installs
  • Q&A populated: Answer common questions before homeowners ask them

Review Velocity: The HVAC Ranking Accelerant

Google's local ranking algorithm heavily weights both review quantity and recency. For HVAC, the optimal review strategy:

  • Text every customer a review request within 2 hours of job completion — 38-42% response rate vs. 8-12% for email
  • Target 5+ new Google reviews per month minimum; 10+ per month to compete in metro markets
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours — signals active management to Google
  • Never review-gate — it violates Google's policies and can result in suspension

Location Pages for Multi-City HVAC Coverage

If you serve multiple cities but your GBP only ranks in one, you're leaving most of your service area's organic traffic to competitors. Create individual location pages for your top 5-10 service cities:

  • Unique title: "HVAC Repair & Installation in [City], [State] | [Company]"
  • City-specific content: mention local neighborhoods, weather patterns, typical equipment age
  • Embed a Google Map showing your service area
  • Include city-specific reviews and testimonials if available

Seasonal GBP Posting for HVAC

GBP posts that align with HVAC seasonal demand generate 30-40% more profile interactions than generic posts:

  • March-April: "AC tune-up specials — beat the summer rush"
  • May-August: Emergency AC repair availability, same-day service
  • September-October: Furnace inspection and tune-up promotions
  • November-February: Emergency heating repair, 24/7 availability
