What Are Google Local Service Ads for HVAC?

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) are the sponsored listings that appear at the very top of Google search results—above regular Google Ads and organic results. They're specifically designed for home service businesses like HVAC contractors.

When someone searches "AC repair near me" or "emergency furnace service", they see LSA results first with the Google Guaranteed badge, star ratings, hours of operation, and a direct call button. These ads are incredibly effective because:

  • Prime positioning: You appear above all other results
  • Trust signals: Google Guaranteed badge builds instant credibility
  • Pay per lead: You only pay when a customer actually contacts you
  • Lower cost per lead: Typically 30-50% cheaper than regular Google Ads clicks

Why LSAs Are Critical for HVAC Contractors

HVAC is one of the most competitive categories in local search. When someone's AC breaks in July or their furnace fails in January, they're searching for help right now. They don't scroll past page one—they call the first trustworthy company they see.

Local Service Ads put you in that #1 position with the Google Guaranteed badge. This combination of visibility and trust is incredibly powerful for HVAC contractors competing against established players.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do HVAC Local Service Ads cost?

HVAC LSAs work on a pay-per-lead model. You only pay when a customer contacts you through the ad (call, message, or booking). Typical lead costs range from $15-50 per lead depending on your market and service type. Emergency calls typically cost more than maintenance leads.

What's the difference between LSAs and regular Google Ads for HVAC?

Local Service Ads appear above regular Google Ads with Google Guaranteed badge, show your Google star rating prominently, and you only pay per lead (not per click). Regular Google Ads give you more control over keywords and messaging but you pay per click regardless of conversion.

How do I get the Google Guaranteed badge for my HVAC company?

To earn Google Guaranteed status, you must pass Google's screening process which includes license verification, insurance verification, and background checks for all technicians. FieldSprout helps you complete the verification process and maintain compliance.

Should HVAC companies pause LSAs during slow seasons?

No. Since you only pay per lead, there's minimal risk in staying active year-round. FieldSprout's AI adjusts your weekly budget down during slow seasons while keeping you visible for emergency repairs and installation inquiries.
