Get More Irrigation Leads

Irrigation has two critical seasonal windows — spring startup and fall winterization — that define your annual revenue. Here's how to capture both peaks, convert seasonal customers into annual recurring clients, and generate new system installation leads year-round.

The Two Irrigation Seasons: Spring and Fall

Irrigation marketing follows a predictable annual calendar. Missing either season means losing customers to competitors who capture them at the decision moment.

Spring Startup Season (March-May)

When temperatures break and homeowners start thinking about their lawns, they need their irrigation systems turned on and inspected. Start Google Ads in late February — early planners book weeks before the first warm weekend. Key terms: "sprinkler startup service," "irrigation turn on near me," "spring sprinkler startup." Offer a spring package that bundles startup with a zone check.

Fall Winterization Season (September-October)

Fall is where you retain spring customers and add new ones. The most efficient fall marketing: an automated reactivation campaign to every customer who did spring startup. 70%+ will book winterization from a simple text or email. For new customers: "Don't let your irrigation system freeze — winterization available now."

Converting Seasonal Customers into Annual Clients

The most valuable irrigation customers are those on annual plans (startup + winterization + repair priority). Convert seasonal customers to annual contracts by:

  • Offering a discounted bundled package at spring startup signup
  • Automatic fall winterization reminder at the right time
  • Annual system inspection and report as a value-add

New System Installation: The High-Value Lead

New irrigation system installs ($2,500-5,000) come from two high-intent audiences: new homeowners and homeowners who recently added landscaping. Target both on Meta Ads with "Complete your outdoor project with a professional irrigation system."
