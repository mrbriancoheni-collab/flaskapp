Why Local Service Ads Are Critical for Irrigation Companies

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) appear at the very top of search results—above regular Google Ads and organic listings. When someone searches "sprinkler repair near me" or "irrigation installation [city]", LSAs are the first thing they see. And unlike Google Ads where you pay per click, LSAs charge per lead (actual phone call or message), making them extremely cost-effective for irrigation contractors.

The Google Guaranteed badge that comes with LSAs provides instant trust—Google has verified your business license, insurance, and background checks. This trust factor is especially important for irrigation services where homeowners are giving you access to their property and underground utilities.

The Irrigation LSA Challenge: Standing Out in a Competitive Market

LSAs show only 2-3 irrigation companies at the top of search results. If you're not in that top group, you're invisible. LSA ranking is determined by several factors—review quality and quantity, response time, proximity to searcher, and Google Guaranteed status. Many irrigation companies struggle because:

  • Low review count — fewer than 10 reviews makes it nearly impossible to rank
  • Slow response times — responding to leads 15+ minutes after they come in hurts ranking
  • Incomplete profiles — missing business hours, service areas, or categories
  • No Google Guaranteed — verification process not completed (required for LSAs)
  • Budget too low — running out of budget early in the week limits lead volume
  • Inaccurate service areas — showing for searches outside profitable zones

Irrigation LSA Best Practices

Profile Optimization Checklist

  • ✅ Complete Google Guaranteed verification (license, insurance, background checks)
  • ✅ 20+ reviews with 4.5+ star average
  • ✅ Accurate business hours (update for seasonal changes)
  • ✅ Professional business photos (team, trucks, completed jobs)
  • ✅ Detailed service area (ZIP codes, not just city-wide)
  • ✅ Relevant service categories (repair, installation, winterization)
  • ✅ Appropriate weekly budget (enough to last full week without running out)

Lead Response Protocol

  1. Respond within 2 minutes — LSA ranking and customer expectations demand speed
  2. Call back via phone — even if lead came via message, call them (higher booking rate)
  3. Qualify the job — confirm location is in service area, understand the problem
  4. Book immediately — give specific appointment time, don't say "we'll call you back"
  5. Send confirmation — text or email with technician name, arrival window, contact info

Review Generation Best Practices

  • Request review immediately after job completion (same day)
  • Use SMS with direct Google review link (easier than email)
  • Personalize message: "Hi [Name], thanks for trusting us with your irrigation system today!"
  • Don't incentivize reviews (violates Google policies)
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours (especially negatives)

Common Irrigation LSA Questions

How much do irrigation LSA leads cost?

Repair leads: $20-50 per lead (varies by market competitiveness)
Installation leads: $50-120 per lead (higher value = higher cost)
Winterization leads: $15-35 per lead (seasonal, high volume)

Unlike Google Ads (pay per click), LSAs charge per actual lead—a phone call lasting 30+ seconds or a message conversation. This makes LSAs more cost-effective because you're not paying for clicks from tire-kickers.

Can I run LSAs and Google Ads simultaneously?

Yes, and you should. LSAs and Google Ads work together:

  • LSAs: High-intent local searches, pay-per-lead, Google Guaranteed badge
  • Google Ads: Broader reach, seasonal campaigns, control over ad copy and landing pages

Most successful irrigation companies run both. LSAs capture the highest-intent local traffic, while Google Ads expand reach for installations, seasonal services, and broader targeting.

How long does Google Guaranteed verification take?

Typical timeline: 3-7 days after submitting all required documents. The process includes:

  • Business license verification (1-2 days)
  • Insurance certificate review (1-2 days)
  • Background checks (3-5 days, sometimes instant)

Delays happen if documents are expired, incomplete, or don't match your business name exactly. FieldSprout's verification checklist prevents common issues that delay approval.

What if I get a bad review on my LSA profile?

Respond professionally and promptly (within 24 hours). A thoughtful response to a negative review can actually build trust with future customers. Address the issue, apologize if appropriate, and explain what you'll do differently.

Don't: Get defensive, argue, or ignore negative reviews. This makes you look unprofessional and hurts both your ranking and conversion rate.

Do: Generate 5-10 new positive reviews to dilute the impact of one negative. A single 1-star review among 50 five-star reviews has minimal impact.
