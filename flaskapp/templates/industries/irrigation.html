Irrigation Marketing FAQs

How do irrigation companies get more customers?

Google Ads during spring startup (March-May) and fall winterization (September-October) drive the most leads. GBP optimization handles year-round repair calls. The key is having strong visibility before peak seasons start — homeowners search in early spring, not after the first hot week.

What's the best marketing strategy for a sprinkler company?

Best results come from: Google Ads for peak season searches, GBP for year-round local visibility, and email/text reactivation campaigns to past customers each season. Past startup customers book winterization at 70%+ rates.

How much does it cost to get sprinkler system leads?

Irrigation leads range $55-110. Spring startup and winterization leads run $55-75 due to high volume. New system installs cost $80-110. With job values of $200-5,000, even high CPL generates strong margins.

How do I get more irrigation system installation jobs?

New installs come from Google Ads targeting "sprinkler system installation cost" and Meta Ads targeting new homeowners. RevForge can target people who recently purchased a home in your service area — the highest-intent audience for new irrigation systems.
