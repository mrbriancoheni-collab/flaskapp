Google Business Profile for Landscaping Companies

Landscaping GBP profiles with weekly project photos receive 3-4x more views than profiles with static content. Your portfolio is your marketing — here's how to turn your GBP into a living visual portfolio that drives both Maps rankings and design-build conversions.

Landscaping GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Landscaper
  • • Secondary: Landscape Designer + Lawn Care Service + Hardscaping Contractor
  • • Photos: Weekly uploads — before/after, in-progress, completed projects
  • • Posts: 2x per week minimum — visual content is king for landscaping
  • • Seasonal: Spring sign-up content in February, fall cleanup in September

Visual Content: The Landscaping GBP Advantage

No home service category benefits more from visual GBP content than landscaping. Every project photo is a portfolio piece that shows prospective clients what you can do:

  • Before/after pairs: overgrown yard vs. finished landscape design; cracked patio vs. new pavers
  • In-progress photos: excavation to grading to planting — shows process and professionalism
  • Seasonal variety: spring plantings, summer lush growth, fall color, winter structure
  • Upload 3-5 photos per week during active season — Google ranks profiles with recent, frequent photo uploads higher

Separating Maintenance and Design-Build in GBP

Maintenance clients and design-build clients need different messaging. Your GBP services section should serve both:

  • Maintenance services: Weekly Lawn Mowing, Lawn Fertilization, Mulching, Spring/Fall Cleanup, Leaf Removal
  • Design-build services: Landscape Design, Planting Installation, Patio Installation, Retaining Walls, Outdoor Kitchens
  • Photos labeled by service type let potential clients find relevant examples of work similar to their project

Spring Sign-Up GBP Campaign

Spring is the critical maintenance sign-up window. Your GBP should be actively marketing from late February:

  • GBP post in late February: "Spring cleanup and weekly maintenance sign-ups now open. Limited routes available."
  • March post: "Before/after spring cleanup — your lawn can look like this"
  • First spring photos of 2025: fresh mulch, new plantings, clean edging — post within 48 hours of completed spring jobs

Design-Build Review Strategy

Design-build project reviews are uniquely valuable — they often include project-specific details that build credibility:

  • Ask for a review at project completion walkthrough while standing in the new space
  • Invite clients to share a photo with their review — photos with reviews significantly boost engagement
  • Design-build reviews mentioning the project type ("new patio," "full backyard design," "water feature") add keyword relevance

Landscaping GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my landscaping Google Business Profile?

Add Landscape Designer, Lawn Care Service, and Hardscaping Contractor as secondary categories, upload 3-5 project photos per week during active season, run spring sign-up posts in late February, and list maintenance and design-build services separately. Visual content drives 3-4x more profile views for landscaping.

What GBP categories should a landscaping company use?

Primary: Landscaper. Secondary: Landscape Designer, Lawn Care Service, Hardscaping Contractor. Each category opens maintenance, design, and hardscaping searches respectively. Use all applicable categories to maximize search coverage.

How do I get more landscaping Google reviews?

After design-build completion at the project walkthrough, at end of first maintenance season, and after spring cleanup transformations. Invite clients to share a photo — photos with reviews are more engaging for future prospects researching your work.

What should landscaping companies post on Google Business Profile?

Before/after project photos, in-progress transformations, spring sign-up posts in late February, seasonal cleanup promotions, and team photos. Post 2x per week during active season — landscaping benefits more from photo content frequency than any other trade.
