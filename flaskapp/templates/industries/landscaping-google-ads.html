Why Landscaping Google Ads Fail Without Trade-Specific Optimization

Landscaping is one of the most searched-for home services on Google — and one of the most wasted Google Ads categories. The problem is that "landscaping" attracts three very different searchers: homeowners ready to hire, DIYers looking for inspiration, and job seekers looking for employment. Without aggressive negative keyword strategies and intent-based campaign structure, 30-45% of your landscaping ad budget goes to people who will never call you.

Beyond waste, landscaping has uniquely valuable campaign types that require separation: maintenance contracts (recurring, lower ticket, high LTV), design-build installs (project-based, $3,000-25,000), and emergency/cleanup services (seasonal, urgent). Mixing these in a single campaign means your ad copy and landing pages can't speak specifically to each buyer's intent.
