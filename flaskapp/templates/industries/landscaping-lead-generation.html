🌿 Landscaping Lead Generation

Get More Landscaping Leads

Landscaping has a compressed spring season and a visual buying decision — two factors that require specific marketing tactics. Here's how to generate landscaping leads that fill your spring schedule 6 weeks before the season and build a base of recurring maintenance clients.

Landscaping Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $60–$130 (maintenance $60-80, design-build $100-130)
  • • Recurring maintenance LTV: $2,000–$5,000/year
  • • Design-build avg job: $5,000–$25,000
  • • Spring signup window: February–May
  • • Best visual channel: Meta Ads + GBP project photos

The Landscaping Lead Calendar

Landscaping is seasonal in a predictable way — and your marketing should match it:

  • January-February: Pre-season awareness. Run low-budget Google Ads and Meta to capture early planners. Start collecting spring project deposits from last year's clients.
  • March-May: Peak season. Maximum budget on Google Ads, LSAs, and Meta. New maintenance contract signings happen here — make recurring service offers prominent.
  • June-August: Maintenance mode. Reduce new acquisition spend, focus on upsells (irrigation, lighting, hardscape additions) to existing clients.
  • September-October: Fall installs and cleanup contracts. Aeration, overseeding, fall planting, and pre-sell for spring projects.
  • November-December: Off-season in northern markets. Holiday lighting installs (high margin, seasonal), and pre-book spring maintenance contracts at discount.

The Recurring Maintenance Acquisition Funnel

The highest-LTV landscaping customers are maintenance contract holders. The most effective funnel:

  1. Google Ads or Meta Ad captures interest → form submission or call
  2. Landing page shows maintenance plan tiers (weekly, biweekly, monthly)
  3. Estimate call includes a discount for annual contract signup
  4. Contract signed, automatic billing starts
  5. Automated annual renewal with upsell offer (add aeration, fertilization, etc.)
