🌿 Landscaping Local SEO

Local SEO for Landscaping Companies

Landscaping is a visual business — homeowners making a $10,000-50,000 design-build decision research extensively online and want to see your work. Local SEO that showcases your portfolio while building Maps visibility captures both maintenance clients and high-value design-build buyers.

Landscaping Local SEO Benchmarks

  • • Visual content impact: Weekly project photos drive 3-4x more GBP views
  • • Best platform: Houzz (design-build) + Google (maintenance)
  • • Design-build vs. maintenance: Different keywords, different buyer intent
  • • Seasonal peak: March-May; invest in SEO 3-4 months prior
  • • Ranking timeline: 90-120 days for competitive markets

Visual SEO: Landscaping's Competitive Advantage

Most home service categories compete on text content and reviews. Landscaping adds a powerful visual dimension that directly influences both rankings and conversions:

  • GBP profiles with 100+ photos receive 3-4x more views than profiles with under 10 photos
  • Weekly photo uploads signal active business to Google's local ranking algorithm
  • Before/after project photos in GBP posts drive direct conversions from profile visitors
  • Location tagging on photos can help GBP appear for neighborhood-specific searches

Separating Maintenance and Design-Build in Local SEO

Landscaping companies that serve both maintenance and design-build clients need separate SEO strategies for each:

  • Maintenance keywords: "lawn maintenance service [city]," "landscape maintenance company near me" — recurring, lower-ticket, higher volume
  • Design-build keywords: "landscape design [city]," "backyard design company," "landscaping company near me" — project-based, higher-ticket, longer research cycle
  • Hardscaping keywords: "patio installation [city]," "retaining wall contractor," "outdoor kitchen installation" — specific project types with high intent

Houzz: The Landscaping-Specific Citation Source

Houzz is the dominant platform for design-build landscaping research. A complete Houzz profile provides:

  • Strong backlink to your website from a high-authority domain
  • Portfolio visibility to homeowners doing large design-build research
  • Houzz reviews that feed into your overall review profile
  • Direct referral traffic from homeowners browsing project photos

Neighborhood Portfolio Pages

Create individual pages for each high-value neighborhood you serve:

  • Title: "Landscaping Services in [Neighborhood] — [City] | [Company]"
  • Include 4-6 project photos with the neighborhood name in image alt text
  • A quote from a local client builds neighborhood-specific social proof
  • These pages rank for hyper-local searches and drive trust when homeowners see their neighbors' yards
