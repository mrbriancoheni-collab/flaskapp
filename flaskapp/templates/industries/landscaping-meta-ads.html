Facebook & Instagram Ads for Landscaping Companies

  • • Meta CPL range: $60-100 (maintenance) / $80-120 (design-build)
  • • Best creative format: Before/after split photos, project walkthrough videos
  • • Best audience: Homeowners, $75k+ HHI, in-service-area ZIP codes

Why Meta Ads Work Uniquely Well for Landscaping

Unlike most trades where emergency or urgent need drives Google searches, landscaping is often a desire-driven decision. Homeowners scrolling Instagram and seeing a stunning backyard transformation start imagining their own yard differently. This is why before/after content and project showcases perform exceptionally well for landscaping on Meta — you're inspiring the desire, not just capturing it.

Meta also offers targeting capabilities Google Ads can't match. You can specifically reach homeowners who have recently moved (highest probability of needing a new landscaper), homeowners in specific ZIP codes, and homeowners of high-value properties — the demographics most likely to invest in professional landscaping.

High-Performing Meta Ad Formats for Landscaping

1. Before/After Transformation Ads

The single best-performing Meta ad format for landscaping. A split image showing an overgrown, neglected yard transformed into a manicured, designed outdoor space generates strong emotional response and high click-through rates. Copy: "See what we did with this [City] backyard in 3 days — free estimate available."

2. Spring Seasonal Promotions

February-April, run "Spring Cleanup Special" and "New Client Discount" promotions targeting homeowners who haven't worked with you before. The seasonality creates natural urgency without manufactured discounting. These campaigns typically see 30-40% lower CPL than year-round brand campaigns.

3. Design-Build Video Walkthroughs

For high-ticket landscape design ($5,000-25,000), 60-second video walkthroughs of completed projects perform well. Show the transformation, the craftsmanship, and briefly explain the design approach. Target homeowners of high-value properties within your service area.

4. Retargeting for Unclosed Estimates

Homeowners who visited your website but didn't request an estimate are your warmest Meta audience. RevForge builds retargeting campaigns that show your best project photos to these visitors for 30 days, keeping you top-of-mind during the decision window.

Landscaping Meta Ads FAQs

Do Facebook Ads work for landscaping companies?

Yes — landscaping is highly visual and Meta is where visual marketing thrives. Before/after transformations and project walkthroughs perform exceptionally well. Meta's homeowner and recently-moved targeting is more powerful than anything Google Ads offers for audience demographics.

What Meta Ads perform best for landscaping?

Best performers: (1) before/after transformation photos, (2) spring special promotions, (3) video walkthroughs for high-ticket projects, (4) retargeting to website visitors. Before/after content consistently generates the lowest CPL at $60-80.

How do I target the right homeowners with landscaping Meta Ads?

Best targeting: homeowners + $75k+ HHI in your service area ZIPs. For design-build, target homes valued $300k+. For new customer acquisition, target "recently moved" homeowners — they need new service providers and are actively making decisions.

How much should a landscaping company spend on Facebook Ads?

Start with $500-1,000/month and scale based on CPL. Landscaping Meta Ads typically generate $60-100 CPL. Meta complements Google Ads — Google captures active searchers, Meta builds awareness with homeowners who haven't started searching yet.
