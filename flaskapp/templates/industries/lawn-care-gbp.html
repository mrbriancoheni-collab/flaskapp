Google Business Profile for Lawn Care Companies

Lawn care is the easiest home service category to generate Google reviews — weekly customer contact, visible results every visit, and a recurring relationship create natural review moments no other trade can match. Here's how to optimize your GBP for spring sign-ups and build the review velocity that dominates your local market.

Lawn Care GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Lawn Care Service
  • • Secondary: Lawn Mowing Service + Landscaper
  • • Spring post: Published in late February — before competitors
  • • Reviews: 8-12 per month in active season (highest achievable in home services)
  • • Post frequency: Weekly during active season (March-November)

Spring Sign-Up GBP Campaign: The Most Important Posts of the Year

Late February GBP posts reach homeowners who are making lawn care decisions before spring arrives. Publishing before competitors is the entire strategy:

  • February 15-20: "Spring 2025 mowing routes now forming in [City]. Weekly and biweekly service available — limited spots."
  • March 1: "Spring cleanup scheduling open. Book now before April fills."
  • March-April: First mow of the season photos — "spring is here, routes are running"
  • Homeowners who book in late February are making a season-long commitment — highest-LTV customers

Lawn Care GBP Services: Recurring and Seasonal

List every service with the recurring-first framing:

  • Weekly Lawn Mowing — "Weekly mowing service with edging and blowing included. Free quote."
  • Biweekly Lawn Mowing — "Every-other-week mowing for smaller lawns and budgets."
  • Fertilization Program — "5-step fertilization program. Thick green lawn all season."
  • Lawn Aeration & Overseeding — "Fall aeration and overseeding for thick spring turf."
  • Spring Cleanup — "Leaf removal, edging, cleanup for season start."
  • Fall Cleanup — "Leaf removal and season-end cleanup."

Review Velocity: The Lawn Care GBP Superpower

Weekly service gives you more review opportunities than any other trade. Systematic review generation builds a Map 3-pack position faster:

  • After 3rd weekly service: text review link — "you've seen 3 great mows, I'd love your quick Google review"
  • Target 2-3 review texts per week during active season — 8-12 per month is achievable
  • Reviews mentioning "weekly service," "always on time," "same crew" add recurring-service keyword relevance
  • High review velocity is the single fastest way to reach Maps 3-pack for lawn care

Aeration and Overseeding GBP Posts: September Revenue

Aeration and overseeding is the most profitable lawn care upsell — $100-200 per client for a September service:

  • August post: "Fall aeration and overseeding scheduling now open. Best time to thicken thin turf."
  • September posts: weekly aeration completion photos showing lawn plugs and fresh seed
  • Before/after of thin turf vs. thick lawn after overseeding is compelling seasonal content
  • Existing customers who book aeration renew at dramatically higher rates the following spring

Lawn Care GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my lawn care Google Business Profile?

Publish spring sign-up posts in late February, list weekly and biweekly mowing as separate services, add aeration/overseeding for fall revenue, text review requests after 3rd weekly service, and post crew and lawn photos weekly during active season.

What GBP categories should a lawn care company use?

Primary: Lawn Care Service. Secondary: Lawn Mowing Service, Landscaper. All three together cover recurring maintenance, mowing service, and landscape service searches. Lawn Mowing Service is the most common consumer search term for recurring service.

How do I get more lawn care Google reviews?

After 3rd weekly service — relationship established, 3 good results delivered. Text within 2 hours of completing the service. Target 8-12 per month in active season — lawn care has more review opportunities per month than any other home service trade.

What should lawn care companies post on Google Business Profile?

Late February spring sign-up posts, weekly crew and lawn photos March-November, August aeration scheduling post, September aeration before/after, and end-of-season wrap-up in November. Spring sign-up posts published before competitors is the single highest-ROI GBP tactic for lawn care.
