Get More Lawn Care Leads

Lawn care has the lowest CPL in the trades ($35-70) and the highest recurring value — a weekly mowing client at $250/month is worth $3,000/year. Here's how to build a spring sign-up campaign that adds dozens of recurring clients before the season even starts.

Lawn Care Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $35–$70 (lowest in the trades)
  • • Recurring client LTV: $2,400–$4,800/year
  • • Spring signing window: March–April
  • • Route density advantage: Same-block clients = 3x profitability
  • • Aeration upsell: $100–$200 per client in September

The Spring Sign-Up Window: Your Biggest Revenue Opportunity

March and April are when most homeowners choose their lawn care service provider for the entire season. Homeowners who are frustrated with their current provider, moving to a new home, or finally deciding to hire out their lawn care are all actively searching in this window. If you're not visible and compelling during this 6-8 week period, you're filling gaps left by attrition for the rest of the year.

Spring campaign strategy:

  • Start marketing in late February — early planners make decisions before the first mow
  • Google Ads peak budget in March-April targeting "lawn care service" and "lawn mowing company near me"
  • Meta Ads spring special — "First mow free with sign-up for weekly service" drives form completions
  • GBP weekly posts in spring showing your crew working and happy clients

Route Density: The Profitability Multiplier

Serving 6 clients on the same block takes 90 minutes. Serving 6 clients scattered across 6 neighborhoods takes 3 hours. Route density is the single biggest driver of lawn care profitability. RevForge's geo-targeting builds route density by concentrating new customer acquisition in neighborhoods where you already serve clients. This approach:

  • Reduces drive time between clients by 40-60%
  • Allows you to take on more clients with the same crew and truck
  • Creates natural word-of-mouth as neighbors see your truck regularly

Converting One-Time Requests into Recurring Contracts

Many lawn care leads start as "just one mow" requests. Convert them to recurring clients:

  • Show subscription vs. per-visit pricing on your landing page (subscription is 15-20% cheaper per visit)
  • During first visit, leave a door hanger or quote for recurring service
  • Follow-up text after first mow: "We'd love to keep your lawn looking this way — recurring service starts at $X/visit"
  • Seasonal transitions: convert one-time cleanups into ongoing maintenance
