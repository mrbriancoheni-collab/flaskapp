Get More Pest Control Leads

Pest control has the lowest CPL in the trades ($40-80) but the highest recurring revenue potential — a service contract customer generates $400-800/year for multiple years. Here's how to build a pipeline that prioritizes high-LTV recurring contracts alongside one-time treatments.

  • • Recurring contract LTV: $400–$800/year
  • • Termite treatment avg: $2,000–$5,000

  • • Negative keywords: "DIY pest control," "how to get rid of," "pest control products"

Why Recurring Contracts Are the Key to Pest Control Profitability

A one-time pest treatment generates $150-350 in revenue. A quarterly recurring service contract generates $400-800/year for 3-5+ years. The math is clear: acquiring a recurring contract customer with a $50 CPL generates $1,500-4,000+ in lifetime revenue. Lead generation strategy for pest control should prioritize contract customers above all else.

The best way to acquire recurring contract customers: lead with a "first treatment free" or deeply discounted offer that requires signing a quarterly contract. The upfront discount is paid back within the first contract period, and the long-term LTV is exceptional.

Seasonal Pest Control Lead Strategy

Spring (March-May): Ants, Bees, Mosquitoes

Spring is the highest-volume season for pest control. Target "ant infestation," "bee nest removal," "mosquito treatment," and "spring pest prevention." This is also the best time to pitch recurring contracts — homeowners thinking about summer mosquito problems will sign annual plans in April.

Summer (June-August): Mosquitoes, Wasps, Bed Bugs

Mosquito treatment and wasp nest removal peak in summer. Bed bug issues spike in summer travel season. Keep consistent spend on these pest-specific campaigns.

Fall (September-November): Rodents, Cockroaches, Spiders

Rodent prevention is your biggest fall opportunity. Mice and rats move indoors as temperatures drop — targeted "mouse exterminator," "rodent prevention," and "rat control" campaigns surge in September-October.

Year-Round: Termite Treatment

Termite treatment is the highest-ticket pest control job ($2,000-5,000). Run separate termite campaigns year-round with specific messaging about structural damage risk and the importance of annual inspections.

Google Ads Negative Keywords for Pest Control

Pest control attracts massive DIY traffic — "how to get rid of ants," "how to kill bed bugs yourself," "DIY pest control spray" — these searches will never call a professional. Block them all with aggressive negatives:

  • "DIY pest control," "do it yourself," "how to get rid of," "how to kill," "home remedy"
  • "pest control products," "pest control spray," "pest control supplies," "traps"
  • "pest control school," "pest control certification," "pest control jobs"
  • Specific product brands (Raid, Orkin Home Defense, etc.)
