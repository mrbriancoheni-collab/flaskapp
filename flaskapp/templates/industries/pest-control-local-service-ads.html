What Are Google Local Service Ads for Pest Control?

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) are the sponsored listings that appear at the very top of Google search results—above regular Google Ads and organic results. They're specifically designed for home service businesses like pest control companies.

When someone searches "exterminator near me" or "termite inspection", they see LSA results first with the Google Guaranteed badge, star ratings, hours of operation, and a direct call button. These ads are incredibly effective for pest control because:

  • Prime positioning: You appear above all other results
  • Trust signals: Google Guaranteed badge builds instant credibility
  • Pay per lead: You only pay when a customer actually contacts you
  • Lower cost per lead: Typically 30-45% cheaper than regular Google Ads clicks

Why LSAs Are Critical for Pest Control Companies

Pest control is competitive in local search, especially during peak seasons (spring/summer). When homeowners discover ants in the kitchen, termite swarmers, or a bed bug infestation, they're searching for immediate solutions. They call the first trustworthy company they see.

Local Service Ads put you in that #1 position with the Google Guaranteed badge. This combination of visibility and trust is powerful when competing for pest control customers against established national brands and local competitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much do pest control Local Service Ads cost?

Pest control LSAs use pay-per-lead pricing. You only pay when a customer contacts you (call, message, or booking). Typical lead costs range from $10-40 per lead depending on your market and pest type. Termite and bed bug leads typically cost more than general pest control.

What's the difference between LSAs and regular Google Ads for pest control?

Local Service Ads appear above regular Google Ads with Google Guaranteed badge, show your Google star rating prominently, and you only pay per lead (not per click). Regular Google Ads give you more control over keywords and pest-specific messaging but you pay per click.

How do pest control companies get the Google Guaranteed badge?

To earn Google Guaranteed status, you must pass Google's screening which includes pest control license verification, insurance verification, and background checks for all technicians. FieldSprout helps you complete the verification process and maintain compliance.

Should pest control companies pause LSAs during slow seasons?

No. Since you only pay per lead, there's minimal risk staying active year-round. FieldSprout's AI adjusts your weekly budget down during winter (for mosquito services) while keeping you visible for termite, rodent, and general pest control inquiries.
