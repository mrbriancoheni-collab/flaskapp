Get More Plumbing Leads

Plumbing is the most emergency-driven trade — 40% of calls happen after hours when pipes burst, drains back up, and water heaters fail. Here's how to build a plumbing lead pipeline that captures emergency calls 24/7 and scales up high-ticket water heater and repiping jobs.

  • • Emergency calls: 40% of all plumbing revenue
  • • Avg repair ticket: $450 | Water heater replacement: $1,200+

  • • No seasonality: Plumbing emergencies happen year-round

Plumbing Lead Generation: Emergency First

Unlike HVAC or landscaping, plumbing has no meaningful seasonality — pipes burst and drains back up year-round. The defining characteristic of plumbing demand is urgency: when a homeowner has water flooding their basement or a clogged drain backing up sewage, they need a plumber immediately. This changes your marketing strategy:

  • Run ads 24/7/365 — not just business hours
  • Keep budgets consistent year-round — no shoulder season for emergencies
  • Response speed is more important than ad spend — first call wins the job

The 4 Highest-ROI Plumbing Lead Channels

1. Google Ads (Emergency + High-Ticket Services)

Structure plumbing Google Ads into three separate campaigns for maximum performance:

  • Emergency Plumbing: "burst pipe repair," "emergency plumber near me," "drain backup," "water heater leaking" — 24/7, highest bids
  • Water Heater Replacement: "water heater replacement cost," "new water heater installation," "tankless water heater" — year-round, high ticket ($1,200+)
  • Routine Services: "drain cleaning," "toilet repair," "sewer line inspection" — business hours, lower bids

Negative keywords: "plumbing school," "plumbing apprenticeship," "DIY plumbing," "plumbing parts," "how to unclog," "plumbing code," "plumbing supply"

2. Google LSAs (24/7 Google Guaranteed)

LSAs are particularly valuable for plumbing because they appear at the very top — above Google Ads — and include the Google Guaranteed badge. For emergency plumbing searches, this badge provides critical trust when a homeowner is in crisis and needs to call immediately. LSA plumbing CPL: $45-90.

3. Google Business Profile (Maps Dominance)

For "plumber near me" searches — the most common plumbing query — the Google Maps 3-pack captures 70%+ of clicks. To rank:

  • 6-10 new reviews per month (velocity matters)
  • 24/7 hours listed accurately
  • Respond to every review within 24 hours
  • Weekly GBP posts showcasing completed jobs

4. Meta Ads (Water Heater and Repiping Campaigns)

While plumbing emergencies can't be predicted, some plumbing work is planned: aging water heaters, known pipe issues, and remodels. Meta Ads targeting homeowners with older homes (pre-2000) generate consistent $50-80 CPL leads for water heater replacement and repiping consultations.

The 2-Minute Rule for Plumbing Leads

Plumbing emergencies have a uniquely short decision window. A homeowner with a burst pipe will call 3-5 contractors and hire whoever answers first. Studies show:

  • Under 1 minute response: 90%+ contact rate
  • Under 5 minutes: 75% contact rate
  • 5-30 minutes: 30% contact rate
  • Over 30 minutes: Under 10% contact rate

Implement instant text acknowledgment for web form submissions and forward-to-cell for all phone calls. Never let a plumbing lead go to voicemail during business hours.
