Frequently Asked Questions

Are Local Service Ads available for pool companies?

Yes, Google Local Service Ads support pool installation and pool service categories in most markets. Check your service area for availability. LSAs provide pay-per-lead pricing and Google Guaranteed badge for pool contractors.

How much do pool LSA leads cost?

Pool LSA leads typically cost $40-100 per lead depending on service type and market. Pool installation leads average $60-120, while pool service/maintenance leads cost $30-70. Pay-per-lead model is often more cost-effective than Google Ads.

What verification is required for pool LSAs?

Pool contractors need contractor license or pool contractor specialty license, general liability insurance ($1M+), business license, and background checks for all technicians. Some states require additional pool-specific certifications or bonds.

How can pool companies improve LSA ranking?

Maintain 4.5+ star Google review rating with 50+ reviews, respond to inquiries within 2-3 minutes, complete business profile with pool installation photos, and keep 90%+ responsiveness rate. Proximity and review quality heavily impact ranking.
