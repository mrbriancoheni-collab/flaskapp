Google Business Profile for Restoration Companies

A homeowner with a flooded basement at 2am is calling the first restoration company that appears in Maps. Your GBP must communicate 24/7 availability, IICRC certification, and insurance claim expertise before a homeowner even visits your website. Here's how to optimize your restoration GBP for emergency dominance.

Restoration GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Water Damage Restoration Service
  • • Secondary: Fire Damage Restoration Service + Mold Remediation Service
  • • Hours: Open 24 Hours — mandatory for emergency restoration
  • • Description: IICRC certification + insurance claim handling + 60-min response
  • • Storm post: Published within hours of any major weather event

24/7 GBP Hours: The Restoration Non-Negotiable

60% of water damage calls happen outside business hours. Your GBP hours must reflect your actual availability:

  • If you handle 24/7 emergency calls: mark GBP as "Open 24 Hours" — this is required for emergency restoration visibility
  • Business description: "24/7 Emergency Water, Fire, and Mold Restoration. On-site within 60 minutes for emergencies."
  • GBP profiles showing 24/7 hours receive significantly more emergency clicks than profiles showing "Closes at 5 PM"
  • Response time promise in description — "60-minute response" or "on-site in under 2 hours" — is a critical call-to-action

IICRC Certification: The Restoration Trust Signal

IICRC is the industry standard certification for restoration work. Insurance adjusters and homeowners specifically look for it:

  • Include "IICRC Certified" in your GBP business description — prominently, not buried
  • Add the IICRC certification logo as a GBP photo
  • Post: "IICRC certification — industry standard for water, fire, and mold restoration. What it means and why it matters."
  • Individual tech certifications (WRT, ASD, AMRT) can be listed in the team/staff section

Damage-Type GBP Services

Each damage type has a separate search pattern and needs its own service listing:

  • Emergency Water Damage Restoration — "24/7 water removal, drying, and restoration. On-site within 60 minutes."
  • Fire and Smoke Restoration — "Fire damage assessment, cleanup, soot removal, and reconstruction."
  • Mold Remediation — "IICRC-certified mold assessment, containment, and removal."
  • Storm Damage Restoration — "Emergency storm damage restoration — water, wind, hail."
  • Sewage Cleanup — "Emergency sewage backup cleanup and disinfection."

Insurance Claim GBP Messaging

60-70% of restoration jobs are insurance claims. Your GBP should lower the barrier to calling:

  • Business description: "We work directly with all major insurance carriers. We handle the full claims process. Most homeowners pay $0 out-of-pocket."
  • Post: "Water damage is covered by most homeowner policies. We handle the claim so you focus on your family."
  • Insurance-specific Q&A in GBP: "Do you work with State Farm?" — Yes, we work with all major carriers.

Storm Event GBP Response: Speed Is Revenue

Every hour after a storm without a GBP post is revenue going to competitors:

  • Pre-draft: "Storm damage in [City area]? Emergency restoration available. We're on-site within [X] hours." Ready to publish in 2 clicks
  • Update your business description temporarily to reference the storm event and your response availability
  • Post before/after storm damage restoration photos within 48 hours of completing jobs

Restoration GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my restoration company Google Business Profile?

Mark 24/7 hours if you offer emergency service, display IICRC certification in description, add separate categories for water/fire/mold, include insurance claim messaging, and have storm event posts pre-drafted to publish within hours of weather events. 24/7 hours is the single most impactful GBP change for emergency restoration.

What GBP categories should a restoration company use?

Primary: Water Damage Restoration Service (highest volume). Secondary: Fire Damage Restoration Service, Mold Remediation Service. Each category makes your profile visible for a different type of restoration emergency search. Water damage is primary; fire and mold are secondary.

How do I get more restoration Google reviews?

After project completion and after insurance settlement confirmation — when the homeowner's relief is complete. Water damage emergency response reviews are especially valuable — "they were on-site in 45 minutes" demonstrates the capability that drives future emergency calls.

What should restoration companies post on Google Business Profile?

Storm event response posts within hours of weather events, before/after water damage restoration photos, "we handle the insurance claim" posts, IICRC certification explanations, and monthly "available 24/7" reminders. Storm event post speed directly impacts the number of storm jobs captured.
