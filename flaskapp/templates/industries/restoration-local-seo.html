24/7 Local SEO: The Restoration Imperative

Restoration is the only home service category where the majority of emergency searches happen at night and on weekends. Your local SEO must communicate 24/7 availability:

  • Set GBP hours to "Open 24 Hours" if you offer 24/7 emergency service — this is the single most impactful GBP change for emergency restoration
  • GBP business description first line: "24/7 Emergency Water Damage and Restoration Services"
  • Emergency response time in description: "On-site within 60 minutes for emergency calls"
  • Your GBP rank for "emergency restoration near me" searches depends on these signals

Separate Pages for Each Damage Type

Water damage, fire damage, and mold remediation have different search patterns, different urgency levels, and different conversion messaging:

  • Water Damage: Emergency urgency, 24/7 messaging, "Stop the damage now" — fastest conversion
  • Fire Restoration: Less immediate, insurance process, timeline expectations — slower, deliberate search
  • Mold Remediation: Health concerns, testing, certification requirements — trust and expertise messaging
  • Each page targets different keywords and converts with different messaging

Insurance Claim Content: The Restoration Conversion Page

60-70% of restoration jobs are paid by insurance. A page dedicated to the insurance process is the highest-converting content on a restoration website:

  • "We work directly with all major insurance carriers — State Farm, Allstate, USAA, and 50+ others"
  • "We handle the entire claims process from documentation to final settlement"
  • "Most homeowners pay $0 out-of-pocket for covered losses"
  • Target keywords: "insurance water damage claim [city]," "restoration company works with insurance"

IICRC Certification: The Restoration Trust Signal

IICRC (Institute of Inspection Cleaning and Restoration Certification) is the industry standard. Homeowners and insurance adjusters look for it:

  • Display IICRC certification prominently in GBP business description
  • Create a page explaining what IICRC certification means and why it matters
  • IICRC directory listing is a strong citation source with domain authority
  • Insurance adjusters favor IICRC-certified contractors — mention this in your content

Storm Event Content: Pre-Built for Fast Deployment

When a major storm hits your market, restoration search volume spikes 5-10x. Pre-built content deploys faster than creating new pages during an event:

  • Draft "Storm Damage Restoration in [City]" page in advance — publish within hours of an event
  • "Flood Damage Restoration [City]," "Hail and Wind Damage Restoration [City]" — ready to activate
  • GBP posts can be drafted in advance and published immediately post-storm
