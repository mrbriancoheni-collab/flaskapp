Restoration Marketing FAQs

How do restoration companies get emergency leads?

Emergency restoration leads come primarily from 24/7 Google Ads and Google Business Profile. The critical factor is being visible and answering immediately — homeowners in crisis call the first company they find that answers. RevForge keeps emergency keywords active 24/7/365.

What's the average cost per lead for restoration companies?

Restoration CPL ranges $90-200. Emergency water damage leads run $90-120; fire and mold leads run $150-200. With average job values of $8,000-15,000, even a $200 CPL delivers exceptional ROI on a $12,000 job.

How should restoration companies market to insurance adjusters?

Adjuster referrals come from LinkedIn outreach, IICRC membership, and preferred contractor networks. For homeowner search, messaging should emphasize "we work directly with your insurance" and "no out-of-pocket cost" to lower the call barrier.

Is Google Ads or Local SEO better for restoration companies?

Both are essential. Google Ads captures people searching right now after damage — the highest urgency. Local SEO builds visibility for homeowners researching mold or planned mitigation.
