Google Business Profile for Roofing Companies

A roofing GBP needs to do two things well: appear for storm damage searches when search volume spikes 500-1000%, and communicate manufacturer certifications and insurance expertise that convert high-value replacement leads. Here's the complete optimization guide.

Roofing GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Roofing Contractor
  • • Secondary: Storm Damage Roofing + Roof Repair Service + Metal Roofing Contractor
  • • Description: GAF/CertainTeed certification + insurance claim messaging
  • • Post-storm: Publish "free inspection" GBP post within 24 hours of event
  • • Reviews: 10+ per month during storm season; surge after job clusters

Storm Damage GBP Category: The Most Critical Roofing Setting

Adding "Storm Damage Roofing" as a secondary GBP category makes your profile eligible to appear for storm-specific searches that spike massively after weather events:

  • Add this category if you handle storm damage insurance claims
  • After a major hail or wind event, your GBP with this category appears for "storm damage roofing near me" and "hail damage roof repair" searches
  • Competitors without this category miss the entire storm-driven traffic spike
  • Pair with a post published within 24 hours of a storm: "Storm damage in [City]? Free inspection available."

Manufacturer Certifications in Your GBP

GAF Master Elite and CertainTeed Select ShingleMaster certifications are compelling trust signals for homeowners selecting a roofer:

  • Include the certification name in your GBP business description
  • Upload a photo of your certification plaque or digital badge
  • Add a GBP post explaining what the certification means: "As a GAF Master Elite contractor, we're authorized to offer the Golden Pledge warranty — the strongest in the industry"
  • Only 2% of roofing contractors achieve GAF Master Elite status — this is a significant differentiator

Insurance Claim Messaging in Your GBP

60-70% of roofing replacement jobs are insurance claims. Your GBP should communicate insurance expertise:

  • Business description: "We work directly with all major insurance carriers — State Farm, Allstate, USAA, Farmers, and more. We handle the full claims process."
  • Service: "Insurance Claim Assistance — we manage documentation, adjuster meetings, and final payment"
  • Post: "Had storm damage? We handle the entire insurance claims process. Most homeowners pay $0 out-of-pocket."

Post-Storm GBP Response Strategy

When a storm hits your market, every hour of GBP response time matters:

  • Have a pre-drafted "storm damage inspection" GBP post ready to publish within hours of an event
  • Update your business description to add "Storm Damage Inspections Available — [Month] [Year]"
  • Add "Storm Damage" to your services section temporarily with appointment availability messaging

Roofing GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my roofing Google Business Profile?

Add Storm Damage Roofing as a secondary category, include GAF/CertainTeed certifications in your description, add insurance claim process messaging, upload project photos by material type, and have a pre-drafted storm post ready to publish within hours of a weather event.

What GBP categories should a roofing company use?

Primary: Roofing Contractor. Secondary: Storm Damage Roofing (most important in storm markets), Roof Repair Service, Metal Roofing Contractor (if applicable). Storm Damage Roofing triggers visibility for searches that spike 500-1000% after weather events.

How do I get more roofing Google reviews?

Post-storm surge: send review requests to all completed storm jobs in 48-72 hours. At final walkthrough when homeowner sees completed roof. Reviews mentioning "hail damage," "storm repair," and "insurance claim" add keyword relevance for future storm searches.

What should roofing companies post on Google Business Profile?

Storm damage before/after photos, "free inspection" post within 24 hours of storms, manufacturer certification explanation, insurance process posts ("we handle the claim"), material-specific posts, and spring inspection reminders. Post response time after storms directly impacts storm job volume.
