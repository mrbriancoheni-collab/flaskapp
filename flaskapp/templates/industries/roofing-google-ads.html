Why Roofing Google Ads Are Different

Roofing contractors face unique Google Ads challenges. Your business has extreme urgency variations—emergency leak repairs require same-day response, while roof replacements have weeks-long consideration periods. Your job values range dramatically from $300 leak repairs to $25,000 full replacements. And your demand spikes dramatically after severe weather events.

Generic Google Ads strategies fail for roofers because they don't account for storm damage surges, the insurance claim process, financing requirements for high-ticket jobs, and the massive difference between emergency repairs and planned replacements.

The Roofing Google Ads Problem: Material Shoppers

Most roofing companies waste 35-55% of their Google Ads budget on people who will never hire a professional roofer. They're DIYers looking for how-to videos, contractors researching material costs, homeowners pricing shingles at Home Depot, and job seekers researching roofing careers.

The most common roofing Google Ads mistakes:

  • No negative keywords for "DIY", "materials", "shingles price", "how to", "jobs"
  • Broad match keywords like "roofing" that attract material shoppers and DIYers
  • Mixing leak repairs and replacements in the same campaigns
  • No storm damage response plan to capitalize on demand surges
  • Generic ad copy that doesn't emphasize financing for high-ticket replacements
  • No insurance claim assistance messaging (major decision factor for homeowners)
