The 4 Key Roofing Lead Channels

1. Google Ads: Replacement, Repair, and Insurance Claims

Three distinct campaigns with different messaging and budgets:

  • Roof Replacement: "roof replacement near me," "new roof cost," "roofing contractor near me" — financing messaging, 2-4 week project timeline
  • Storm Damage & Insurance: "hail damage roof," "storm damage roof inspection," "insurance roof claim" — scale immediately post-storm
  • Roof Repair: "roof leak repair," "missing shingles repair," "emergency roof repair" — faster decision, smaller ticket

Key negative keywords: "roofing jobs," "roofing apprenticeship," "DIY roof repair," "roofing materials," "roofing school," "roofing supplier"

2. Post-Storm Meta Ads (Timing is Everything)

Immediately after a storm event, Meta Ads are extraordinarily effective because homeowners are emotionally activated and haven't started searching yet. A well-timed ad ("Did your home survive last night's hail storm? Free damage inspection this week") within 24 hours of a storm generates $60-100 CPL leads with very high close rates. RevForge monitors weather data and triggers these campaigns automatically.

3. Google LSAs for Insurance Claim Work

The Google Guaranteed badge is particularly valuable for insurance claim roofing because insurance companies and homeowners need to trust the contractor. LSA roofing CPL runs $70-150 — higher than other trades but justified by $8,000-15,000 average job values.

4. Neighborhood Canvassing as a Lead Multiplier

For storm damage work, door-to-door canvassing in affected neighborhoods is the highest-converting physical marketing. Combine digital marketing with canvassing by using Meta Ads geo-targeted to specific neighborhoods hit by storms, then following up with in-person door knocking. Digital + physical in the same neighborhood within 48 hours post-storm converts at 15-25%.

Insurance Claim Roofing: Different Marketing Strategy

Insurance claim roofing (where the insurance company is paying) requires completely different messaging:

  • "We handle the entire insurance process for you"
  • "No out-of-pocket cost (in most cases)"
  • "We work with all major insurance carriers"
  • "Free storm damage inspection — we document everything"

Create separate landing pages for insurance claim leads vs. standard replacement leads — conversion rates improve 30-40% with this separation.
