Why Roofing Companies Need Facebook & Instagram Ads

Google Ads capture homeowners with active roof problems ("roof leak", "emergency roofer"). Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) reach homeowners before emergencies—targeting aging roofs, building awareness after storms, and generating leads for high-value roof replacements ($10,000-25,000) through financing campaigns.

Meta Ads excel at:

  • Roof replacement campaigns with financing for aging roofs
  • Post-storm free inspection offers and insurance claim assistance
  • Seasonal roof maintenance and inspection campaigns
  • Material upgrade campaigns (metal roofing, architectural shingles)
  • Building brand awareness for when roof problems do occur

Roofing Meta Ads Strategy: What to Promote

1. Roof Replacement with Financing Campaigns

Best timing: Year-round, emphasis on spring (Mar-Jun)
Offer example: "New Roof from $149/Month – 0% Financing for 60 Months"
Target audience: Homeowners 45-70 in neighborhoods with homes 15-30 years old
Ad creative: Before/after roof replacement, monthly payment callout, material options

This is your highest-value offer. A $15,000 roof replacement justifies spending $150-250 per lead. Emphasize financing to overcome the sticker shock—most homeowners don't have $15,000 cash but can afford $149/month.

2. Post-Storm Free Inspection Campaigns

Best timing: Immediately after major storms (24-72 hours)
Offer example: "Free Storm Damage Inspection – We Work With Insurance"
Target audience: Homeowners in affected ZIP codes
Ad creative: Storm damage photos, insurance claim process, urgency messaging

After hail storms, high winds, or hurricanes, launch immediate campaigns offering free inspections. Homeowners may not realize they have roof damage until it's inspected. Position yourself as the insurance claim expert.

3. Aging Roof Awareness Campaigns

Best timing: Year-round
Offer example: "Is Your Roof 15+ Years Old? Get a Free Inspection"
Target audience: Neighborhoods where homes were built 1990-2010
Ad creative: Signs of aging roof, replacement timeline, home value protection

Most homeowners don't think about their roof until it fails. Create awareness around roof lifespan (15-25 years for asphalt shingles) and encourage proactive replacement before catastrophic failure.

4. Material Upgrade Campaigns (Metal, Architectural Shingles)

Best timing: Spring/Summer
Offer example: "Upgrade to Metal Roofing – Never Replace Your Roof Again"
Target audience: Higher-income homeowners ($100K+), long-term homeowners
Ad creative: Metal roof benefits (50+ year life, energy savings, curb appeal), financing

Target homeowners interested in premium materials. Metal roofing and architectural shingles command higher prices and better margins while offering clear value propositions (longevity, aesthetics, energy efficiency).

Common Roofing Meta Ads Mistakes

Mistake #1: Promoting Emergency Leak Repairs

Facebook and Instagram users aren't actively searching for emergency roofers. Save that for Google Ads. Use Meta for planned replacements and awareness.

Mistake #2: Showing Total Cost Instead of Monthly Payment

"$15,000 Roof Replacement" scares people away. "$149/Month for New Roof" is much more palatable. Always lead with financing.

Mistake #3: Not Targeting Aging Roof Neighborhoods

Broad targeting wastes budget. Focus on neighborhoods where homes are 15-30 years old (roofs need replacement soon).

Mistake #4: Not Acting Fast After Storms

Storm damage creates massive opportunity. Launch campaigns within 24-48 hours of major storms to capture demand before competitors.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do Facebook Ads work for roofing companies?

Yes, especially for planned roof replacements and storm damage awareness. Facebook and Instagram ads work best for targeting homeowners with aging roofs (15+ years old), post-storm inspection offers, and financing campaigns for roof replacements. Less effective for emergency leak repairs—use Google for that.

What's a good cost per lead for roofing Meta Ads?

Roofing Meta Ads typically generate leads for $18-45 each, significantly cheaper than Google Ads. However, these leads have lower urgency and longer sales cycles (25-40% booking rate vs 35-50% for Google). Focus on nurturing Meta leads with storm damage urgency and financing benefits.

Should roofers advertise on Instagram or just Facebook?

Use both. Instagram works well for reaching younger homeowners (30-45) with visual before/after content of roof replacements and storm damage repairs. Facebook performs better for older homeowners (50+) and detailed financing/warranty information.

What should roofers advertise on Facebook?

Focus on planned roof replacements with financing ($149/month), post-storm free inspections, seasonal roof maintenance, and targeting homes with aging roofs (15-20+ years). Emphasize material options, warranties, and insurance claim assistance. Avoid emergency leak messaging—use Google for that.
