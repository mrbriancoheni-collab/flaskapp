Google Business Profile for Solar Companies

Solar buyers spend 60-90 days researching before making a call. Your GBP needs to communicate the 30% ITC, financing availability, and installation quality throughout this research cycle. Here's how to optimize your solar GBP to capture buyers at the moment of decision.

Solar GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Solar Energy Equipment Supplier
  • • Secondary: Solar Energy Contractor + EV Charging Station Installer
  • • Description: 30% ITC + $0 down financing + local installer differentiator
  • • Posts: ITC deadline content, utility rate updates, completed system photos
  • • Reviews: Process-focused — detail timeline and post-install support

Solar GBP Description: Addressing the 60-90 Day Research Cycle

Solar buyers read GBP profiles more carefully than buyers in other categories. Your description should answer their primary questions immediately:

  • "30% federal tax credit available on qualifying residential solar installations"
  • "$0 down financing available — own your system from day one"
  • "[X]-year installation warranty + [Y]-year panel performance guarantee"
  • "Licensed solar contractor — [X] systems installed in [State] since [year]"
  • Include NABCEP (North American Board of Certified Energy Practitioners) certification if you have it

Solar GBP Services: Cover the Full Product Line

  • Residential Solar Installation — "Complete rooftop solar systems for homes. Free site assessment."
  • Battery Backup Installation — "Whole-home battery backup: Tesla Powerwall, Enphase IQ Battery, Franklin."
  • Solar + EV Charger Bundle — "Charge your EV with clean energy from your own roof."
  • Commercial Solar Installation — "Commercial rooftop and ground-mount solar for businesses."
  • Solar System Monitoring — "24/7 system monitoring and performance optimization."

ITC Year-End GBP Posts: Creating Urgency

The 30% federal ITC must be claimed in the year of installation. This creates a natural year-end urgency window:

  • October post: "Install before December 31 to claim your 30% federal tax credit for 2025"
  • November post: "Last chance for [year] tax credit — permitting takes [X] weeks"
  • December post: "Year-end installation complete — customer saves $[X] in tax credits"
  • This urgency-based content drives Q4 conversions from buyers who have been researching all year

Solar GBP Photos: Building Trust for a $30,000 Decision

Solar is the highest-ticket residential home service. Photo content needs to build confidence in installation quality:

  • Completed system photos on multiple roof types — shingle, tile, metal — so buyers can find their match
  • Installer photos showing certification and safety equipment — professionalism on the roof
  • Monitoring app screenshots showing production and savings data
  • Before/after utility bill comparisons (with permission) — most compelling proof of ROI

Solar GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my solar company Google Business Profile?

Include 30% ITC and $0 down financing in your description, add EV Charger Installer as a secondary category, list battery backup as a service, post ITC deadline content in Q4, and ask reviews to describe the installation timeline and post-install support — the questions solar buyers research most.

What GBP categories should a solar company use?

Primary: Solar Energy Equipment Supplier. Secondary: Solar Energy Contractor, Electric Vehicle Charging Station Installer. Solar + EV charger is a high-value bundled sale — the EV category captures homeowners planning both.

How do I get more solar Google reviews?

At system activation and again at 3-month mark when first bill comparison is available. Ask reviews to describe the installation process and timeline — process-focused reviews are most valuable for solar's long research cycle. Target 2-4 per month — solar has fewer jobs but each review carries more weight.

What should solar companies post on Google Business Profile?

Completed system photos on various roof types, ITC deadline posts in Q4 (October-December), utility rate increase news posts, battery backup availability, and monthly production data from completed systems. Year-end ITC urgency posts drive more conversions than any other solar GBP content.
