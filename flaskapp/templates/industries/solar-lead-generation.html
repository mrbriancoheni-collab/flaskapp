Get More Solar Leads

Solar has the highest CPL and the longest sales cycle in the trades — but also the highest average job value ($25,000-35,000). Here's how to build a solar lead pipeline that leverages tax credit urgency, targets high-utility-bill homeowners, and nurtures 60-90 day decisions into closed installs.

Solar Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $120–$300 (highest in the trades)
  • • Avg system value: $25,000–$35,000
  • • 30% ITC reduces cost to: $17,500–$24,500 after credit
  • • Sales cycle: 60–90 days (long nurture required)
  • • Best target: Homeowners with $200+/month electric bills

Solar Lead Generation: ITC-Driven Marketing

The 30% Investment Tax Credit (ITC) — extended through 2032 by the Inflation Reduction Act — is the most powerful sales tool in the solar industry. Every solar lead generation campaign should lead with this: "Install solar before year-end and claim a 30% federal tax credit." For a $28,000 system, that's $8,400 back at tax time. This is concrete, verifiable, and highly motivating for qualified homeowners.

Targeting the Right Solar Prospects

Not every homeowner is a solar prospect. The highest-converting solar leads share these characteristics:

  • Homeowners (not renters) — they need to own the roof
  • High electric bills ($200+/month) — the economics work better
  • South-facing roof with minimal shading (Meta can't target this, but your sales process filters it)
  • Good credit if financing — solar loans require 680+ typically

Meta Ads can specifically target homeowners in your service area with interests in energy costs, sustainability, or home improvement. Include "Are you a homeowner paying $200+/month in electricity?" as a qualifier in your ad copy — it pre-qualifies leads and improves close rate significantly.

The 60-90 Day Solar Sales Funnel

Unlike emergency trades where leads close same-day, solar leads take 60-90 days on average. Your marketing must support a longer nurture sequence:

  1. Week 1: Initial contact → instant savings estimate → schedule site assessment
  2. Weeks 2-4: Site assessment → custom proposal → follow-up calls and emails
  3. Weeks 4-8: Proposal review → financing options → HOA/utility coordination
  4. Weeks 8-12: Contract signing → permit submission → installation scheduling

Build retargeting campaigns on Meta that show different content at each stage of this funnel — reminders about ITC deadlines, financing terms, and energy savings calculators for unconverted leads.
