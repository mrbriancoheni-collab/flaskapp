Local SEO for Solar Companies

Solar buyers spend 60-90 days researching before getting a quote. Local SEO that answers cost, financing, and tax credit questions throughout this cycle builds trust and captures buyers when they're ready to call — not just when they're first looking.

Solar Local SEO Benchmarks

  • • Research cycle: 60-90 days before first call
  • • National competition: Sunrun, SunPower — compete on local trust
  • • High-value content: ITC page + utility savings calculator
  • • Battery backup: Fast-growing, lower-competition keyword
  • • Ranking timeline: 120-180 days for competitive markets

Solar's Long Research Cycle: Local SEO That Nurtures Buyers

Solar is not an impulse purchase. Buyers spend weeks comparing companies, calculating ROI, and understanding incentives before getting a single quote. Local SEO for solar needs to capture buyers at every research stage:

  • Early research: "How much does solar cost in [State]?" — educational content
  • Financial analysis: "Solar ROI on [Local Utility] bills" — utility-specific savings content
  • Tax credit research: "30% solar tax credit 2025" — ITC content with local application
  • Company selection: "Best solar company in [City]" — reviews and GBP visibility

Competing With National Solar Companies on Local SEO

Sunrun, SunPower, and other nationals dominate broad solar keyword rankings. Independent installers compete on local specificity:

  • Utility-specific content: "Solar on [Local Utility] — Net Metering Explained" targets buyers in your exact service area
  • Local permitting knowledge: "Solar Permit Process in [City/County]" positions you as the local expert
  • Local installer reviews emphasizing responsiveness and post-install support — nationals often fail here
  • Average response time to inquiries is measurably faster for local installers — highlight this

Tax Credit Content: The Highest-Converting Solar SEO Page

The 30% federal Investment Tax Credit is the most compelling financial argument for solar. A page dedicated to it captures buyers who are financially motivated:

  • Title: "Federal Solar Tax Credit 2025 in [State] — How It Works"
  • Include a simple calculator showing the tax credit on your average system price
  • Address state-level incentives specific to your market
  • FAQ: "Can I use the solar tax credit if I don't owe taxes?" — answers the #1 ITC question

Battery Backup: The Emerging Low-Competition Opportunity

Home battery backup searches (Tesla Powerwall, Enphase IQ Battery, Franklin Whole Home) are growing 50-80% annually with lower competition than solar installation:

  • Dedicated battery backup page: "Home Battery Backup Installation in [City]"
  • Brand-specific pages: "Tesla Powerwall Installation [City]," "Enphase Battery Installation [City]"
  • Combine with solar: "Solar + Battery Backup Systems in [City]"
