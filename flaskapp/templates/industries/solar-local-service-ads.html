Local Service Ads for Solar: Current Status and Opportunity

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) have been a game-changer for home service businesses like HVAC, plumbing, and electrical contractors. The pay-per-lead model and prominent Google Guaranteed placement generate high-quality leads at predictable costs. However, LSAs for solar installation are not universally available yet.

As of 2024, Google is gradually rolling out Local Service Ads for solar installation companies in select markets. Availability varies significantly by state and metro area. Some regions have full LSA access for solar, while others are still waiting for the category to launch.

Why this matters: If LSAs are available in your market, they represent one of the most cost-effective solar lead generation channels. If they're not available yet, it's critical to prepare now so you're ready to dominate when LSAs launch in your area.

Why Solar Companies Should Prioritize LSAs (Where Available)

For solar contractors lucky enough to have LSA access, the platform offers significant advantages over traditional Google Ads:

Pay-Per-Lead vs. Pay-Per-Click

In competitive solar markets, Google Ads can cost $12-25 per click. At a 6% conversion rate, that's $200-400 per lead. Local Service Ads charge a fixed $50-120 per lead regardless of how much competition exists. You only pay when someone actually contacts you through the platform—not for accidental clicks, bot traffic, or tire-kickers who bounce immediately.

Top-of-Search Placement

LSAs appear above all Google Ads and organic results, giving you prime visibility when homeowners search "solar companies near me" or "solar panel installation." This top placement is especially valuable on mobile, where the LSA section dominates the entire first screen.

Google Guaranteed Trust Badge

The green Google Guaranteed checkmark signals to homeowners that you're verified, licensed, and backed by Google's guarantee. For high-ticket purchases like solar installations ($15,000-30,000), this trust factor significantly increases contact rates.

Mobile-First Lead Capture

Solar searches increasingly happen on mobile devices. LSAs are optimized for one-tap calling and messaging, reducing friction between search and contact. Homeowners can reach you in seconds without visiting a website or filling out forms.

Solar LSA Verification Requirements

Getting Google Guaranteed for solar installation requires more verification than most home services due to the complexity and licensing requirements. Here's what you'll need:

Business Licensing and Insurance:

  • State contractor license: General contractor or specialized solar installation license (requirements vary by state)
  • Electrical license: Required in many states for solar work (master electrician or solar-specific electrical license)
  • Roofing license: Some states require roofing contractor license since solar involves roof penetrations
  • General liability insurance: $1 million minimum coverage (many solar contractors carry $2M+)
  • Workers compensation: Proof of workers comp insurance for all employees

Background Checks:

  • Business owner background check
  • Background checks for all installers who interact with customers
  • Ongoing background check requirements for new hires

Business Verification:

  • Physical business address (no P.O. boxes)
  • Business phone number verification
  • Business website (optional but recommended)
  • Years in business documentation

Solar-Specific Documentation:

  • Proof of solar installation experience (portfolio of completed projects)
  • Manufacturer certifications (Tesla Powerwall, Enphase, SolarEdge, etc.)
  • Industry certifications (NABCEP certification preferred but not always required)

Solar LSA vs. Traditional Google Ads: Comparison

Should solar companies use LSAs instead of Google Ads, or both? Here's the breakdown:

Use Local Service Ads for:

  • Cost predictability: Fixed $50-120 per lead regardless of click costs
  • Top visibility: Appear above all Google Ads
  • Mobile-first leads: One-tap calling from search results
  • Trust building: Google Guaranteed badge for high-ticket solar sales
  • Budget efficiency: No wasted spend on unqualified clicks

Use Traditional Google Ads for:

  • Service area expansion: Target areas 30-60 miles away where LSA coverage is thin
  • Specific services: Solar battery storage, commercial solar, solar maintenance
  • Educational content: Target "solar cost", "solar incentives" research queries
  • Remarketing: Re-engage website visitors who didn't convert
  • Brand building: Display ads and YouTube for broader awareness

Best strategy: Use both channels in coordination. Allocate 50-60% of budget to LSAs (where available) for high-intent local leads, and 40-50% to Google Ads for service area expansion and remarketing.

Preparing for LSAs If Not Yet Available in Your Market

If Local Service Ads for solar aren't available in your area yet, start preparing now:

1. Build Your Google Review Base

Start systematically requesting Google reviews from every solar installation. When LSAs launch in your market, you want to already have 50-100 reviews at 4.5+ stars. Competitors starting from zero reviews will take 6-12 months to catch up.

2. Verify All Licensing and Insurance

Ensure your contractor licenses, electrical licenses, and insurance policies are current and properly documented. The LSA verification process can take 5-10 business days, so having everything ready accelerates your launch.

3. Optimize Response Processes

Train your sales team to respond to inquiries within 2-3 minutes. Set up mobile notifications, call forwarding, and CRM systems that alert reps immediately when leads arrive. Practice on your current Google Ads and organic leads.

4. Monitor LSA Availability

Check Google's Local Services platform monthly to see if solar has launched in your market. Sign up for early access if Google offers it. Being one of the first solar companies in LSAs gives you a massive first-mover advantage.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Google Local Service Ads available for solar companies?

Google Local Service Ads for solar installation are available in select markets but not universally rolled out. Availability varies by state and region. Check Google's LSA platform to see if solar is available in your service area.

What verification is required for solar LSAs?

Solar contractors need state contractor license verification, general liability insurance ($1M+), business license, background checks for all installers, and proof of solar installation experience. Some states require additional electrical or roofing licenses.

How much do solar Local Service Ads cost per lead?

Solar LSA leads typically cost $50-120 per lead where available. This pay-per-lead model is often more cost-effective than pay-per-click Google Ads, especially in competitive markets where CPC can reach $15-25.

How can solar companies improve their LSA ranking?

Maintain a 4.5+ star Google review rating with 50+ reviews, respond to inquiries within 2-3 minutes, complete your business profile with photos and certifications, and maintain 90%+ responsiveness rate. Proximity to searcher also affects ranking.
