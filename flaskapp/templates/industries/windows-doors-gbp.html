Google Business Profile for Window & Door Companies

Window replacement buyers research for 2-4 weeks and are strongly motivated by the 30% IRA tax credit and energy savings. Your GBP should communicate financial incentives, showcase window styles visually, and build the trust needed for a $3,500-8,000 commitment.

Window & Door GBP Optimization Checklist

  • • Primary category: Window Installation Service
  • • Secondary: Window Supplier + Door Supplier + Door Installation Service
  • • Description: 30% IRA tax credit + ENERGY STAR + financing available
  • • Photos: Window styles by type — organized for visual browsing
  • • Q4 posts: ITC year-end urgency content (October-December)

IRA Tax Credit GBP Content: The Most Powerful Financial Message

The 30% federal IRA tax credit on energy-efficient windows is the strongest conversion tool in window replacement marketing. Your GBP should lead with it:

  • Business description: "Energy-efficient window replacement qualifies for 30% federal tax credit in 2025. ENERGY STAR certified products available."
  • Q4 post series (October-December): "Install before December 31 to claim your 2025 tax credit. Average homeowner saves $1,200-1,800 in tax credits."
  • Year-round post: "30% federal tax credit available on qualifying window replacement — ask about eligible products"
  • Tax season (February-April): "Use your tax refund for window replacement — qualify for next year's credit too"

Window Style Photos: The Visual Shopping Experience

Homeowners browsing window replacement options want to see styles before calling. Organize your GBP photo gallery like a visual catalog:

  • Double-hung windows — the most common residential replacement style
  • Casement windows — popular for ventilation and modern aesthetics
  • Bay and bow windows — premium replacement with dramatic visual impact
  • Picture windows — large, fixed windows for views
  • Sliding windows — common in certain regions and home styles
  • Before/after pairs showing old foggy or drafty windows vs. new clear modern installations

Separate Window and Door GBP Services

Window and door buyers are different people with different motivations. Service them separately:

  • Window Replacement — "Energy-efficient double and triple pane window replacement. 30% tax credit available."
  • Entry Door Replacement — "Fiberglass and steel entry door installation. Security, insulation, and curb appeal."
  • Sliding Door Replacement — "Patio and sliding door replacement. Energy-efficient and secure."
  • Storm Door Installation — "Storm door installation for added insulation and protection."

Financing GBP Messaging

A $5,000 window project is a significant purchase. Financing messaging reduces the call hesitation:

  • Business description: "$0 down financing available. Own your new windows for as low as $89/month."
  • Post: "New windows, $0 down. Finance your energy-efficient upgrade — qualify in minutes."
  • Monthly payment examples make the total price feel more manageable for comparison shoppers

ENERGY STAR Partner GBP Content

ENERGY STAR certification is required for the IRA tax credit. Display it prominently:

  • Add ENERGY STAR partner logo as a GBP photo
  • Post explaining ENERGY STAR certification: "What is ENERGY STAR and why it matters for your window tax credit"
  • Description: "ENERGY STAR certified window products — qualify for 30% federal tax credit"

Window & Door GBP FAQs

How do I optimize my window company Google Business Profile?

Include 30% IRA tax credit in your description, add Window Supplier and Door Supplier as secondary categories, organize photos by window style, add $0 down financing messaging, and run Q4 ITC urgency posts from October through December.

What GBP categories should a window company use?

Primary: Window Installation Service. Secondary: Window Supplier, Door Supplier, Door Installation Service. Window Supplier captures research-phase buyers. Door Supplier and Door Installation Service cover the separate door replacement search journey.

How do I get more window company Google reviews?

Day of installation completion when homeowners see the improvement. Reviews mentioning energy savings ("drafts are gone") and style ("love the new look") are compelling conversion content for future researchers. Target 4-6 per month. Ask customers to include the window style for keyword relevance.

What should window companies post on Google Business Profile?

Before/after installation photos by window style, IRA tax credit posts year-round (surge in Q4), ENERGY STAR certification explanations, financing availability posts with monthly payment examples, and spring replacement specials. Q4 ITC urgency posts (October-December) are the highest-converting window GBP content of the year.
