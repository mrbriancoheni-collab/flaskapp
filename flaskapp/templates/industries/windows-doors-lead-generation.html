Get More Window & Door Replacement Leads

Window and door replacement is a planned, high-ticket home improvement purchase — with a 2-4 week decision window and powerful financial incentives including the 30% IRA tax credit. Here's how to generate $3,500-8,000 replacement project leads at $80-160 CPL.

Windows & Doors Lead Benchmarks

  • • CPL range: $80–$160 (full-house replacement higher)
  • • Avg job value: $3,500–$8,000
  • • IRA tax credit: 30% (up to $600 for windows)
  • • Decision window: 2–4 weeks
  • • Best Meta target: Homeowners of pre-1990 homes

The IRA Tax Credit: Your Most Powerful Lead Generation Tool

The Inflation Reduction Act's 30% federal tax credit on energy-efficient window replacements (up to $600) is a compelling financial argument that your competitors may not be leveraging. Include it in every touchpoint:

  • Google Ads: "Save 30% with the Federal Tax Credit — Window Replacement Near You"
  • Meta Ads: "2025 window replacements qualify for up to 30% federal tax credit. Is your home eligible?"
  • Landing pages: Prominently show the tax credit calculator and net cost after credit
  • GBP posts: Weekly "tax credit available" posts in Q4 to create year-end urgency

Targeting Homeowners Who Need New Windows Most

Meta Ads offer audience targeting that Google Ads can't match for window replacement. Specifically:

  • Homeowners of older homes: Pre-1990 construction is a proxy for aging single-pane windows
  • High electric bill indicators: Homeowners interested in energy efficiency, utility savings
  • New homeowners: Often discover old windows during first winter or after an energy audit

Financing as a Conversion Tool

A $5,000 window replacement is a significant purchase. Showing "as low as $89/month" or "0% financing for 24 months" in your ads typically improves conversion rates by 20-30% by reducing the psychological barrier of the total price. Include financing options prominently in ads, landing pages, and estimate presentations.

Separate Window vs. Door Campaigns

Window replacement and door replacement attract different buyers with different motivations:

  • Windows: Energy efficiency, drafts, condensation, failed seals, energy savings
  • Entry Doors: Security, curb appeal, insulation, damage
  • Patio Doors: Sliding vs. French, security, energy efficiency, outdated look
