Why Window & Door Companies Should Prioritize LSAs

Google Local Service Ads (LSAs) are one of the most cost-effective lead generation channels for window and door replacement contractors. The pay-per-lead model eliminates wasted spend on accidental clicks, and the Google Guaranteed badge builds instant trust for high-ticket home improvement projects.

For window and door companies, LSAs offer several key advantages over traditional Google Ads:

Pay-Per-Lead vs. Pay-Per-Click Savings

In competitive markets, Google Ads for "window replacement near me" can cost $6-15 per click. At a 10% conversion rate, that's $60-150 per lead. Local Service Ads charge a fixed $35-65 per lead regardless of competition intensity. You only pay when someone actually contacts you—no wasted spend on tire-kickers who bounce immediately.

Top-of-Search Placement

LSAs appear above all Google Ads and organic results, giving you prime visibility when homeowners search for window or door replacement. This top placement is especially valuable on mobile, where LSA listings dominate the entire first screen.

Google Guaranteed Trust for High-Ticket Sales

Window and door replacement projects cost $5,000-15,000, making homeowners cautious about which contractor to hire. The green Google Guaranteed checkmark signals that you're verified, licensed, insured, and backed by Google's guarantee—dramatically increasing contact rates for high-ticket purchases.

Mobile-First Lead Capture

Most window and door searches happen on mobile. LSAs are optimized for one-tap calling and messaging, reducing friction between search and contact. Homeowners can reach you in seconds without filling out forms or visiting websites.

Window & Door LSA Verification Requirements

Getting Google Guaranteed for window and door installation requires verification of licenses, insurance, and background checks:

Business Licensing and Insurance:

  • Contractor license: General contractor license or specialty remodeling/window installation license (varies by state)
  • Business license: City or county business license
  • General liability insurance: $1 million minimum coverage
  • Workers compensation: Required if you have employees

Background Checks:

  • Business owner background check
  • Background checks for all installers who visit customer homes
  • Ongoing background checks for new hires

Business Verification:

  • Physical business address verification
  • Business phone number verification
  • Years in business documentation
  • Portfolio of completed window/door installations (optional but recommended)

Google heavily weights response time in LSA ranking. Window and door companies that respond within 2-3 minutes consistently rank higher than competitors who take 10+ minutes—even if competitors have better reviews.

Window & Door LSA vs. Traditional Google Ads

Should window and door companies use LSAs instead of Google Ads, or both?

Use Local Service Ads for:

  • Cost efficiency: Fixed $35-65 per lead vs. variable $60-150 per lead on Google Ads
  • Top visibility: Appear above all Google Ads and organic results
  • Mobile-first leads: One-tap calling from search results
  • Trust building: Google Guaranteed badge for high-ticket window/door projects
  • High-intent local searches: "window replacement near me", "door installation near me"

Use Traditional Google Ads for:

  • Service area expansion: Target areas 30-60 miles away where LSA costs are higher
  • Specific products: "Pella windows", "Andersen doors", "fiberglass entry doors"
  • Research-phase keywords: "window replacement cost", "best replacement windows"
  • Remarketing: Re-engage website visitors who didn't convert
  • Seasonal promotions: "Spring window sale", "holiday door discounts"

Best strategy: Use both channels in coordination. Allocate 50-60% to LSAs for high-intent local leads, and 40-50% to Google Ads for broader reach and remarketing.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are Local Service Ads available for window and door companies?

Yes, Google Local Service Ads support window installation and door installation categories in most markets. Check your specific service area for availability. LSAs provide pay-per-lead pricing and Google Guaranteed badge placement.

How much do window replacement LSA leads cost?

Window and door LSA leads typically cost $25-70 per lead, depending on your market. Window replacement leads average $35-65, door installation leads cost $30-60. Pay-per-lead pricing is often more cost-effective than pay-per-click Google Ads.

What verification is required for window/door LSAs?

Window and door contractors need general contractor license or specialty remodeling license, general liability insurance ($1M+), business license, and background checks for installers. Some states require additional certifications or bonds.

How can window companies improve LSA ranking?

Maintain 4.5+ star Google review rating with 50+ reviews, respond to inquiries within 2-3 minutes, complete business profile with installation photos, and keep 90%+ responsiveness rate. Proximity to searcher also impacts ranking.
