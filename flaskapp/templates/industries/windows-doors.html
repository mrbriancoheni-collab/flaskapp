Windows & Doors Marketing FAQs

How do window replacement companies get more leads?

Leads come from Google Ads targeting "window replacement near me" and "new windows cost," GBP for local map visibility, and Meta Ads for homeowners in older homes. The energy efficiency angle and federal tax credit create urgency that converts browsers into callers.

What's the average cost per lead for window replacement?

Window/door CPL ranges $80-160. Entry door leads run $80-110. Full-house window replacement leads run $120-160 but produce $5,000-8,000 jobs. RevForge optimizes toward high-ticket replacement searches, not repair queries.

How do I use energy tax credits to get more window jobs?

The IRA provides a 30% federal tax credit (up to $600) for qualifying energy-efficient windows. Include "up to 30% tax credit" in ad copy and landing pages. RevForge includes IRA tax credit messaging automatically — this typically improves CTR by 15-25%.

When is the best time to market window and door replacement?

Spring (March-May) and fall (September-October) are peak seasons. Tax season (February-April) also drives inquiries. RevForge auto-adjusts budgets to these seasonal patterns and uses tax refund timing in ad copy.
