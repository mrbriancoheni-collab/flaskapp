How to set it up in Google Ads

  1. 1 In Google Ads, go to Tools & Settings → Assets → Lead Forms
  2. 2 Create or edit a Lead Form asset. Under Lead delivery, choose Webhook.
  3. 3 Paste the URL above into the Webhook URL field. Google will send a test request to verify it.
  4. 4 Click Test — Google will fire a GET to /verify on your URL and confirm it returns "OK".
  5. 5 Save and attach the Lead Form to your campaigns. Leads will now appear in the table below.
