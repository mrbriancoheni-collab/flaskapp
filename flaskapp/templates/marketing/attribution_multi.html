First Touch

100% of credit goes to the first channel that touched the lead. Best for measuring top-of-funnel awareness channels.

Last Touch

100% of credit goes to the final channel before conversion. Standard model — best for direct response channels.

Linear

Credit split equally across every touchpoint. Most balanced view when channels work together to win a job.
