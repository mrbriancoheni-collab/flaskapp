How to use pricing intelligence

Underpriced signals appear when a service type's average ticket is significantly below your overall average while volume is high — suggesting customers are saying yes easily because your price is low.

Inconsistent pricing flags services with wide ticket spreads, which usually means technicians are quoting differently without a standard process.

What to do: Pick one underpriced service, raise the price by 10%, and track close rate over 30 jobs. If close rate drops by more than 20%, the market won't bear it. If close rate holds, you've found margin.
