Typical seasonal patterns

  • 🔥 HVAC: Surge May–Jul (AC) & Sep–Nov (heat)
  • 💧 Plumbing: Steady year-round; spike Jan (freeze) & Mar (spring thaw)
  • 🏠 Roofing: Peak Apr–Jun post-storm season
  • ⚡ Electrical: Steady; slight surge Dec (holiday lighting)
  • 🌿 Landscaping: March–October; pause Nov–Feb
  • 🐛 Pest Control: Surge Apr–Sep; drop Nov–Feb

Planning tips

  • Increase digital spend 4–6 weeks before your peak season, not when demand hits.
  • Use slow months (typically Jan–Feb) for direct mail and brand building — lower ad costs.
  • Seasonal LSA budgets should track closely with demand — FieldSprout adjusts these automatically.
  • Offline channels (billboard, mail) need 6–8 weeks lead time — plan ahead.
