Why NAP Consistency Matters for Rankings
Consistent NAP = trust signal. Google uses citation data from across the web to verify your business is legitimate and where it says it is.
More citations = more ranking signals. Each directory listing is a vote that your business exists at that location — more votes improve map pack position.
Inconsistencies = ranking penalty. Different phone numbers or addresses across directories confuse Google's algorithm and actively suppress your local rankings.
Duplicates split your authority. Two listings for the same location divide your review signals and citation weight — hurting both.
