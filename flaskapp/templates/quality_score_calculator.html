How This Calculator Works

Google's Quality Score system applies a CPC multiplier relative to a "base" CPC at QS 5. The multiplier table (QS 1 = 5x base cost, QS 5 = 1x, QS 10 = 0.5x) is derived from Google's published documentation on Ad Rank and Quality Score pricing.

This calculator back-calculates your implied base CPC from your current QS level, then applies the target QS multiplier to find your new CPC. The savings projection assumes constant click volume — in reality, lower CPCs at the same budget buys more clicks, so actual savings compound further.

QS improvements in real accounts typically take 30–90 days as Google updates its historical data. Improvements above QS 8 require sustained high CTR and landing page experience scores, which depend on your industry's benchmark CTR.
