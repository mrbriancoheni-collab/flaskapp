How This Planner Works

Monthly multipliers represent relative demand for each trade by climate region, based on Google Trends seasonal patterns and aggregate RevForge account data. A multiplier of 1.5 means demand is 50% higher than the annual average that month; 0.7 means 30% below average.

Budget allocation: each month's share = its multiplier ÷ sum of all 12 multipliers × annual budget. This ensures exact totals. For trades without specific multiplier data (Pest Control, Garage Door, Pool Service, Solar, Other), plumbing multipliers are used as a conservative baseline — these trades tend to be more demand-flat year-round.

Climate regions: Southern/Warm (FL, TX, AZ, CA, GA, NV) — peak HVAC in summer; Northern/Cold (MN, WI, MI, NY, OH) — peak in heating season; Mixed/Moderate (CO, NC, VA, OR, IL) — balanced demand curves. If your market spans both, use Mixed as a starting point.
