What is GEO?

Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) is the practice of ensuring your brand gets cited when AI systems like ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Claude answer questions. Unlike traditional SEO, GEO requires being a well-documented, credibly cited brand — not just a high-ranking page.

  • Publish authoritative content cited by other sites
  • Get listed in industry directories and review platforms
  • Earn press mentions and backlinks from credible sources
  • Use structured data so AI systems can easily extract facts about your business
