What is Google Local Services Ads?

LSA is a separate Google product from Google Ads — most contractors (and most agencies) don't know the difference.

What it is

A separate Google program where you pay per call or message — not per click. Google verifies your license, insurance, and background, then awards you the Google Guaranteed badge. It shows on Google Search, Maps, and the Google Home Services hub.

How it differs from Google Ads

Google Ads charges per click — whether or not the person calls you. LSA charges per verified lead (call or message). You don't bid on keywords. Google matches you to searches based on your service type, location, reviews, and bid. No ad copy. No keyword lists.

What the badge means to homeowners

The Google Guaranteed badge tells homeowners that Google has verified your license and insurance, and that Google will back jobs up to $2,000 if something goes wrong. That guarantee is why LSA leads convert at a higher rate than standard ad clicks.
