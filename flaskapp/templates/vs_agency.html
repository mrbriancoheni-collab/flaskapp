Why agencies struggle with home service ads

Agencies aren't bad at marketing — they're just not built for the speed and specificity that home service Google Ads requires.

One account manager, 30+ clients

Your account gets 30–60 minutes of attention per month. Optimization happens when it's convenient, not when opportunity arises.

Storm hits Tuesday — they update bids Friday

The 72-hour window after a hailstorm is critical for roofers and restoration companies. Agencies miss it. FieldSprout doesn't.

Black-box reporting

Monthly PDFs show impressions and clicks — but not which campaigns actually generated jobs. You're guessing at ROI.

Retainer incentives don't align with yours

Agencies earn the same whether your CPL is $80 or $200. FieldSprout only succeeds when you get better results.

Generalist knowledge, not trade-specific

Most agencies serve e-commerce, SaaS, and home services with the same playbook. Our AI is built exclusively for trades businesses.

Locked into long contracts

6–12 month agency contracts mean you're stuck even if results disappoint. FieldSprout is month-to-month — no commitment needed.

When does a traditional agency make sense?

We'll be honest: agencies have their place. Here's when you might still want one alongside (or instead of) FieldSprout.

Full creative production

Video ads, professional photography, brand identity work — agencies have creative teams. FieldSprout focuses on paid search optimization, not production.

Multi-channel strategy

If you need coordinated TV, radio, billboards, and digital — an agency coordinates all channels. FieldSprout specializes in Google and Facebook paid ads.

Enterprise complexity

50+ locations with custom franchise agreements, complex attribution, and dedicated account teams. Some agencies specialize here — FieldSprout handles up to ~20 locations well.
