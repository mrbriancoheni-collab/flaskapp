    How This Estimator Works

    Waste rates are estimated from industry benchmarks and RevForge account data. Base waste rate is determined by account age: new accounts lack historical data for Google's algorithm to optimize (≈42% waste), while mature, well-optimized accounts still carry ≈22% structural waste from auction dynamics.

    Additional waste factors: Missing negative keywords (+8%) — without blocking irrelevant searches, trade accounts attract DIY searches, competitor searches, and wrong-service queries. 24/7 bidding (+7%) — most trade calls happen 7am–7pm; off-hours clicks rarely convert but still cost. Missing conversion tracking (+5%) — Google's algorithm optimizes blind, wasting spend on sessions it can't learn from.

    Waste is capped at 58% (absolute ceiling for any managed account) and floored at 15% (unavoidable market friction). The chart segmentation is a reasonable approximation; exact waste attribution requires real account data from our free Ads Grader.
