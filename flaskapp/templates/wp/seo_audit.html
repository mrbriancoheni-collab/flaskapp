What is AEO (Answer Engine Optimisation)?

AEO optimises your content to be cited and surfaced by AI-powered answer engines — Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT, Perplexity, Bing Copilot, and voice assistants.

Unlike traditional SEO (which targets ranked blue links), AEO targets zero-click answers — where the AI summarises your content directly without the user clicking through.

  • Direct answers — concise, definition-style intro paragraphs
  • FAQ structure — question headings + FAQPage JSON-LD schema
  • Short paragraphs — 2–4 sentences so AI can extract cleanly
  • Structured lists — bullet points and numbered steps
  • Freshness signals — visible published/updated dates

A page can rank well for SEO but score poorly for AEO — optimising for both maximises visibility across both traditional and AI-powered search.
